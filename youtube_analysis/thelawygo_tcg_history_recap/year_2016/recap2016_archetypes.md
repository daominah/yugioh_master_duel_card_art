# 2016 TCG: Top Archetypes

Summary of [recap2016_transcript.md](recap2016_transcript.md)
(TheLawYGO, The 2016 Yugioh TCG Recap).

Top archetypes are ordered by how long they stayed a top deck, longest first.
Months are approximate: the video dates set releases and ban lists exactly,
but most events only relative to them ("two weeks later").

2016 sets in release order, for reference:
Breakers of Shadow (January 15th), Emperor of Darkness (January 29th),
Wing Raiders (February 12th), Premium Gold: Infinite Gold (March 18th),
Millennium Pack (April 15th), Shining Victories (May 6th), Rise of the True Dragons (July 8th),
Dark Side of Dimensions Movie Pack (July 22nd), The Dark Illusion (August 5th),
Dragons of Legend Unleashed (August 19th),
Structure Decks Yugi Muto and Seto Kaiba (October 21st), In Vengeance (November 4th),
Destiny Soldiers (November 18th).

World Championship 2016 winner (Orlando, August): Blue-Eyes, Shunsuke Hiyama (Japan),
his second title in a row (heard as "Shinsuke Hayame", spelling checked externally).
Worlds used the combined TCG and OCG ban list,
so Blue-Eyes' short TCG top period does not contradict the title.

## Performapal Dracoslayer pendulum

- Top period: January to August (about 8 months),
  as one lineage that changed name with each ban list:
  Performapal Performage ("Pepe"), then Dracoslayer Performapal ("Draco Pals"),
  then Majespecter Dracoslayer Performapal
  (the Majespecter partner package; standalone Majespecter has its own section below).
- New strong support:
  - Breakers of Shadow: `Performapal Monkeyboard`, `Performapal Guitartle`,
    `Performapal Pendulum Sorcerer`, `Master Pendulum, the Dracoslayer`,
    `Dinoster Power, the Mighty Dracoslayer`, `Draco Face-Off`.
  - Shining Victories: `Performapal Odd-Eyes Light Phoenix`, `Performapal Odd-Eyes Unicorn`.
  - Majespecter, mainly `Majespecter Unicorn - Kirin`, became the default partner by June.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Tellarknight Ptolemaeus` into `Cyber Dragon Infinity`, and `Traptrix Rafflesia`
    with `Treacherous Trap Hole`: the rank four toolbox that made Performapal Performage tier zero.
  - `Performage Plushfire` and `Performage Damage Juggler` (the Performage engine),
    `Wavering Eyes`, `Armageddon Knight` with `Blackwing - Zephyros the Elite` (the DARK variant).
  - `Guiding Ariadne` searching `Solemn Strike` or `Solemn Warning` when popped.
  - `Number 38: Hope Harbinger Dragon Titanic Galaxy` (Infinite Gold)
    off the level eight Dracoslayers.
  - `Sky Iris`, which searches the new Odd-Eyes scales.
- Results:
  - Performapal Performage was tier zero: 90% of the known top cut at YCS Sydney (January),
    then the most top 32 spots one deck had ever taken at YCS Atlanta (February), winning both.
  - Dracoslayer Performapal won YCS Prague, YCS Vegas, YCS San Jose and YCS Houston
    (February to April),
    just over 40% of tops after the February adjusted list.
  - Majespecter Dracoslayer Performapal won the South American WCQ,
    and Majespecter Traptrix Performapal won the European WCQ (June to July).
- Ended by: three ban lists.
  February 8th (Plushfire, Damage Juggler, Ptolemaeus banned),
  April 11th (Ignister Prominence, Wisdom-Eye, Draco Face-Off limited),
  August 29th (Monkeyboard banned, Kirin and Pendulum Call limited).
  The pendulum slot then passed to Metalfoes.

## Kozmo

- Top period: January to August (about 8 months).
  Already at the top from late 2015, it adapted into several variants:
  Tin Can Turbo, Demise Kozmo, Fire King Kozmo, Speedroid Kozmo.
- New strong support:
  - Breakers of Shadow: `Kozmo Tincan`, `Kozmo Soartroopers`, `Kozmo Delta Shuttle`,
    `Kozmo Dark Eclipser`, `Kozmojo`.
  - Premium Gold: Infinite Gold: reprinted the whole archetype, making it the budget option.
  - Shining Victories: `Kozmoll Dark Lady`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Emergency Teleport` for the level one pilots (the reason for its April semi-limit).
  - `Call of the Haunted` and `Oasis of Dragon Souls` reviving ships dumped by Tincan
    (Tin Can Turbo).
  - `Card of Demise` (Millennium Pack): draw three after setting backrow (Demise Kozmo).
  - `Fire King Island` (Shining Victories), searchable with `Terraforming`:
    it pops ships in hand to start the deck's lines (Fire King Kozmo).
  - `Artifact Scythe` with `Artifact Sanctum`, and `Anti-Spell Fragrance`,
    against the pendulum swing back.
- Results: second place at YCS Prague (February),
  13 of the top 32 at YCS Providence (May),
  won YCS Rimini with Fire King Kozmo (August).
- Ended by: the August 29th ban list
  (`Kozmo Dark Destroyer` and `Emergency Teleport` limited).

## Burning Abyss with Phantom Knights

- Top period: March to October (about 8 months).
- New archetype and support:
  - Wing Raiders: the Phantom Knights archetype,
    `The Phantom Knights of Ancient Cloak`, `The Phantom Knights of Silent Boots`,
    `The Phantom Knights of Ragged Gloves`, `The Phantom Knights of Break Sword`,
    `Phantom Knights' Fog Blade`.
    The package gave Burning Abyss rank three access to `Dante, Traveler of the Burning Abyss`.
  - Premium Gold: Infinite Gold: `Beatrice, Lady of the Eternal`,
    which made reaching Dante the deck's main goal.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Fiendish Rhino Warrior` (Breakers of Shadow): protects the Burning Abyss self-destruction
    and acts as a Foolish Burial.
  - `Speedroid Terrortop` and `Speedroid Taketomborg`: reach Dante for Beatrice
    without using the normal summon, leaving it for `Tour Guide From the Underworld`.
  - `Chaos Hunter` (stops banishing, so no ABC summons)
    and `Ghost Reaper & Winter Cherries` (reveals Dante, ABC or Spirit Dragon) in the
    YCS Liverpool winning list.
- Results: debuted at YCS Vegas (March), top four at YCS Houston (April),
  then won YCS Santiago (April), YCS Providence (May), YCS Origins (June),
  YCS Toronto (August) and YCS Liverpool (October), with top eight at Worlds (August).
- Ended by: ABC, Metalfoes and Paleozoic pushing it out of the top four by December.
  The August 29th ban list only limited Beatrice and Cir and left Dante untouched,
  which started the "Dante is ban list immune" meme.

## Monarch

- Top period: February to August (about 7 months).
- New strong support:
  - Emperor of Darkness: `Edea the Heavenly Squire`, `Eidos the Underworld Squire`,
    `Aether, the Evil Empowering Dragon`, `Erebus the Underworld Monarch`,
    `Pantheism of the Monarchs`, `The Prime Monarch`, `Domain of the True Monarchs`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Super Quantum Red Layer`, with `Super Quantum Blue Layer` and `Emergency Teleport`
    to search it: special summonable tribute fodder.
  - `The Prime Monarch` revives as a level five, so the Extra Deck variant
    could make `Constellar Pleiades`.
  - `Brilliant Fusion` into `Gem-Knight Seraphinite` (from May):
    an extra normal summon and another level five; later `Dark Witch` from the deck.
  - `Quickdraw Synchron` into `Ultimaya Tzolkin` into `Crystal Wing Synchro Dragon`
    (Shining Victories), later `Blackwing - Gofu the Vague Shadow` (The Dark Illusion).
  - `Majesty's Fiend` against the pendulum swarm (Domain variant).
- Variants: Domain Monarch (empty Extra Deck), then the stronger Extra Deck Monarch
  (from YCS Vegas in March), then Burning Abyss Monarch.
- Results: won the Central America WCQ and the Oceania WCQ (June),
  won the North American WCQ with Domain Monarch,
  taking 21 of the top 64 there (July).
- Ended by: the August 29th ban list (`The Monarchs Stormforth` and other Monarch cards limited).

## Majespecter

- Top period: May to October (about 6 months) as its own deck,
  alongside its role as the partner package in Dracoslayer Performapal.
  It topped events but never won one on its own:
  the 2016 wins with Majespecter cards were Dracoslayer Performapal variants.
- New strong support:
  - Breakers of Shadow: `Majespecter Toad - Ogama` (sets a Majespecter spell or trap from deck)
    and `Majespecter Sonics`. A minor wave, but Ogama searches either response pool.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Card of Demise` (Millennium Pack): give up the pendulum summon for the turn
    to draw into more Majespecter backrow (Demise Majespecter).
  - `Sky Iris`: completes the scales and keeps `Majespecter Unicorn - Kirin` on the board.
  - `Ties of the Brethren`: level threes into `Majespecter Cat - Nekomata`
    and `Majespecter Raccoon - Bunbuku`, level fours into `Majespecter Crow - Yata`
    and `Majespecter Fox - Kyubi`, giving access to the whole spell and trap suite.
- Results: a couple of Majespecter variants topped YCS Providence (May),
  Demise Majespecter debuted at YCS Origins (June),
  top four at Worlds (August), where Kirin's repeatable bounce gave it long game play
  after the spell and trap lines ran out,
  and a new Ties of the Brethren variant topped YCS Minneapolis (October).
- Ended by: the August 29th ban list limiting `Majespecter Unicorn - Kirin` and Pendulum Call.
  It kept topping into October, then lived on as the Majespecter package
  inside Metalfoes (YCS Rio, November).

## Metalfoes

- Top period: September to December (about 4 months).
- New archetype and support:
  - The Dark Illusion: `Metalfoes Steelen`, `Metalfoes Silverd`, `Metalfoes Goldriver`,
    `Metalfoes Volflame`, `Metalfoes Fusion`, `Metalfoes Counter`.
  - In Vengeance: `Raremetalfoes Bismugear`, `Fullmetalfoes Alkahest`,
    `Metalfoes Mithrilium`, `Fullmetalfoes Fusion`,
    which raised it to tier one or two and made it the main pendulum deck.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Qliphort Scout` searching `Qliphort Monolith` (Metalfoes Qliphort),
    then `Cyber Dragon Nova` into `Cyber Dragon Infinity`.
  - `Blackwing - Gofu the Vague Shadow`: two tokens to pop and a level five tuner
    for `Ultimaya Tzolkin`.
  - `Odd-Eyes Meteorburst Dragon` as a scale eight for `Sky Iris`.
  - `Jowgen the Spiritualist`: blocks the whole Blue-Eyes normal summon lineup.
  - From In Vengeance on: the Majespecter package, `Luster Pendulum, the Dracoslayer`
    and `Painful Decision`.
- Results: won YCS Mexico City as Metalfoes Qliphort (September),
  won YCS Minneapolis (October), rose at YCS Rio (November),
  won YCS Bochum, the last YCS of the year (December).

## Blue-Eyes

- Top period: August to October (about 3 months).
  Legacy support for the original `Blue-Eyes White Dragon`.
- New strong support:
  - Shining Victories: `Sage with Eyes of Blue`, `Dragon Spirit of White`,
    `The White Stone of Ancients`, `Blue-Eyes Spirit Dragon`, `Blue-Eyes Twin Burst Dragon`.
    Still rogue: no reliable way to reach Spirit Dragon.
  - Dark Side of Dimensions Movie Pack: `Blue-Eyes Alternative White Dragon`
    (the missing piece for turn one Spirit Dragon), `Neo Blue-Eyes Ultimate Dragon`,
    `Blue-Eyes Chaos MAX Dragon`.
    The speaker suspects the set was rushed to the TCG to make this legal for Worlds.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Effect Veiler`, a level one LIGHT tuner that `Sage with Eyes of Blue` searches.
  - `Return of the Dragon Lords` (Rise of the True Dragons) and `Cards of Consonance`.
  - `The Melody of Awakening Dragon`, which searches Alternative and the original Blue-Eyes.
  - `Advanced Ritual Art` for Chaos MAX.
  - `Azure-Eyes Silver Dragon`, `Black Rose Moonlight Dragon` and `Stardust Spark Dragon`
    as Spirit Dragon's tag out targets.
- Results: Blue-Eyes mirror in the Worlds finals, won by Shunsuke Hiyama (August).
  Missing from YCS Rimini's top 32 under the TCG list,
  then the front-runner at YCS Mexico City after the ban list (September).
- Ended by: ABC at YCS Liverpool (October).

## ABC

- Top period: October to December (about 3 months).
  Legacy support for Kaiba's Battle City XYZ Union monsters.
- New archetype (Structure Deck: Seto Kaiba): `A-Assault Core`, `B-Buster Drake`,
  `C-Crush Wyvern`, `ABC-Dragon Buster`, `A-to-Z-Dragon Buster Cannon`,
  `Union Hangar`, `Union Scramble`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - Level four machines with existing support,
    like `Gold Gadget`, `Silver Gadget` and `Photon Thrasher`, to overlay the equipped Unions.
  - Rank four bosses `Gear Gigant X` and `Bujintei Tsukuyomi`.
  - `Artifact Scythe` with `Artifact Sanctum` (YCS Liverpool),
    `Dimensional Barrier` (YCS Rio winning list),
    `Brilliant Fusion` to dump LIGHT Union pieces (YCS Anaheim winning list).
- Results: 14 of the top 32 at YCS Liverpool (October),
  won YCS Rio (November) and YCS Anaheim (December),
  one of the top four decks at YCS Bochum (December).

## Paleozoic

- Top period: November to December (about 2 months).
- New archetype and support:
  - The Dark Illusion: `Paleozoic Olenoides`, `Paleozoic Hallucigenia`, `Paleozoic Canadia`,
    `Paleozoic Pikaia`, `Paleozoic Anomalocaris`. Not enough on its own yet.
  - In Vengeance: `Paleozoic Eldonia`, `Paleozoic Dinomischus`, `Paleozoic Marrella`,
    `Paleozoic Leanchoilia`, `Paleozoic Opabinia`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Toadally Awesome` (In Vengeance): a rank two omni negate,
    reached through the Frog engine on the first turn. The main reason the deck topped.
  - `Waboku`: protects summoned Paleozoic and triggers one in the grave.
  - `Reckless Greed`: several copies stack without stacking the drawback,
    and each one revives a Paleozoic.
  - `Pot of Desires` and `Maxx "C"`, the only non-trap cards besides the Frogs.
  - Floodgates and counters: `Anti-Spell Fragrance`, `Floodgate Trap Hole`,
    `Quaking Mirror Force`, `Barrier Statue of the Torrent`.
- Results: first top at YCS Rio (November), one of the top four decks at YCS Bochum (December).

## HERO (Elemental HERO, Masked HERO)

- Top period: December (about 1 month).
- New strong support: none of its own.
  The Destiny HERO support in Destiny Soldiers did not change its position.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Toadally Awesome` (In Vengeance) through `Bahamut Shark`,
    ending on `Masked HERO Dark Law` plus Toadally Awesome.
  - `Tin Goldfish` as the other half of Bahamut Shark with `Elemental HERO Bubbleman`.
- Results: tier three at YCS Anaheim, one of the top four decks at YCS Bochum (December).

## Honorable mentions

Got strong new cards and were playable, but never became a top deck in 2016.

### Lightsworn

- New strong support: `Fairy Tail - Snow` (The Dark Illusion),
  fuel for `Minerva, the Exalted Lightsworn`.
- Results: undefeated in Swiss at YCS Rimini,
  tied for second most represented at YCS Liverpool.
- Held back by: Minerva's price, since it was a YCS prize card.

### Mermail Atlantean

- New strong support: `Neptabyss, the Atlantean Prince` (Breakers of Shadow).
- Results: rogue contender from YCS Prague, rising to tier two around YCS Mexico City.

### Shiranui (Zombie)

- New strong support: `Shiranui Solitaire` (The Dark Illusion), one card access
  to `PSY-Framelord Omega` and `Trishula, Dragon of the Ice Barrier` hand loops.
- Results: popular and had to be accounted for, but stayed rogue.

### Raidraptor

- New strong support: `Rank-Up-Magic Soul Shave Force` (Wing Raiders),
  `Raidraptor - Ultimate Falcon` and `Rank-Up-Magic Skip Force` (Shining Victories).
- Results: rogue to tier three, held back by Kaiju.

### Darklord

- New archetype (Destiny Soldiers): `Darklord Ixchel`, `Darklord Tezcatlipoca`,
  `Darklord Morningstar`.
- Results: two tops on its first outing at YCS Anaheim,
  but too many bricks for single elimination.

### D/D

- New strong support: `D/D Savant Thomas` (The Dark Illusion),
  `D/D/D Flame King Genghis` (Mega Tin).
- Results: first top 32 at YCS Liverpool.

### Legacy support that stayed rogue

- Buster Blader: `Buster Blader, the Destruction Swordmaster` (Breakers of Shadow).
- Dark Magician: `Magician of Dark Illusion`, `Dark Magical Circle` (The Dark Illusion),
  `Eternal Soul` (Legendary Decks II).
- Magnet Warrior: `Berserkion the Electromagna Warrior` (Structure Deck: Yugi Muto),
  outclassed by ABC from the same week.
