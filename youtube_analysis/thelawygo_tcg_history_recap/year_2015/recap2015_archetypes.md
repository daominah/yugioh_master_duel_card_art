# 2015 TCG: Top Archetypes

Summary of [recap2015_transcript.md](recap2015_transcript.md)
(TheLawYGO, The 2015 Yugioh TCG Recap).

Top archetypes are ordered by how long they stayed a top deck, longest first,
then by the earlier start month.
Months are approximate: the video dates set releases and ban lists exactly,
but most events only relative to them ("two weeks later").

| Archetype     | Top period      | Months | Highlights                                              |
|---------------|-----------------|--------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| Burning Abyss | Jan to Sep      | 9      | Not legal at Worlds (TCG exclusive at the time)         |
| Tellarknight  | Jan to Aug      | 8      | World Champion (Shunsuke Hiyama)                        |
| Nekroz        | Feb to Aug      | 7      | Tier zero (Feb to Jul), World Championship second place |
| Shaddoll      | Jan, Apr to Jul | 5      |                                                         |
| Infernoid     | Jun to Oct      | 5      |                                                         |
| Qliphort      | Jan to Apr      | 4      |                                                         |
| Kozmo         | Aug to Nov      | 4      |                                                         |
| HERO          | Aug             | 1      | World Championship top 8                                |

World Championship 2015 winner (Kyoto, Aug 15th to 16th): Tellarknight,
Shunsuke Hiyama (Japan; heard as "hayama Shuki" and "hayama shinuki",
spelling checked externally).
He took the spot of the suspended 2014 champion Sehabi Kheireddine
(heard as "sahabi kandine") as the 2014 runner-up.
Worlds used the combined TCG and OCG ban list:
Burning Abyss was TCG exclusive and could not play,
and Shaddoll was crippled by OCG hits,
so the second place Nekroz (Galileo De Obaldia) was the main TCG deck in the top cut.

2015 sets in release order, for reference (TCG English dates):
Secrets of Eternity (Jan 16th), HERO Strike Structure Deck (Jan 30th),
The Secret Forces (Feb 13th),
Premium Gold: Return of the Bling (Mar 20th, the video says Feb 20th),
World Superstars (Apr 17th, the video says Apr 14th), Crossed Souls (May 15th),
Dragons of Legend 2 (Jul 17th), Clash of Rebellions (Aug 7th),
Synchron Extreme Structure Deck (Aug 28th), High-Speed Riders (Oct 2nd),
Dimension of Chaos (Nov 6th), Master of Pendulum Structure Deck (Dec 4th).

Ban lists: Jan 1st, Apr 1st, Jul 16th (the first not on the first of a month)
and Nov 9th.

## Burning Abyss

- Top period: Jan to Sep (about 9 months).
  Already the top deck by the Jan 1st ban list, having overtaken Shaddoll,
  and the deck that kept winning around Nekroz.
- New strong support:
  - Secrets of Eternity: `Farfa, Malebranche of the Burning Abyss`,
    `Libic, Malebranche of the Burning Abyss`, `Cagna, Malebranche of the Burning Abyss`,
    and `Good & Evil in the Burning Abyss` (searches `Fire Lake of the Burning Abyss`).
  - Crossed Souls: `Draghig, Malebranche of the Burning Abyss`
    and `Barbar, Malebranche of the Burning Abyss`, which closes out games.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Mask Change II` into `Masked HERO Dark Law` (HERO Strike),
    three main deck copies in the YCS Prague winning list.
  - `Number F0: Utopic Future` (World Superstars), a rank three body that holds Dante.
  - `Crush Card Virus` after its errata, against Nekroz.
  - `Karma Cut`, `Phoenix Wing Wind Blast`, `Raigeki Break`
    to trigger the Malebranche on the opponent's turn (North American WCQ).
  - `Absolute King Back Jack` with Draghig (YCS Toronto, YCS Monterrey).
  - `Speedroid Terrortop` and `Speedroid Taketomborg` (High-Speed Riders):
    free rank three access to `Dante, Traveler of the Burning Abyss`.
- Results: won YCS Charleston (Jan), YCS Prague (Mar),
  the North American WCQ (Jun), YCS Toronto (Aug) and YCS Monterrey (Sep).
- Ended by: no single hit.
  The Nov 9th ban list limited `Graff, Malebranche of the Burning Abyss`
  and semi-limited `Cir, Malebranche of the Burning Abyss`,
  but left Dante alone, starting the "Dante is ban list immune" meme;
  the video stops reporting its results after Sep.

## Tellarknight

- Top period: Jan to Aug (about 8 months), mostly tier two to three.
- New strong support:
  - Secrets of Eternity: `Stellarknight Constellar Diamond`,
    made from `Stellarknight Delteros` and built to stop Shaddoll and Burning Abyss.
  - Crossed Souls: `Tellarknight Ptolemaeus`, which ranks up into `Constellar Pleiades`
    or makes Diamond from two materials.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Star Seraph Scepter` and `Star Seraph Sovereignty` ("stick and chair")
    and `Galaxy Soldier` (World Superstars) were tried for rank five access.
- Results: tier two to three at YCS Charleston (Jan),
  a surprising resurgence at YCS Chicago (Apr),
  World Champion with Shunsuke Hiyama (Aug),
  as the one Duelist Alliance strategy with no substantial hits on the combined list.

## Nekroz

- Top period: Feb to Aug (about 7 months), tier zero from release
  until the Jul ban list, then still tier one.
- New archetype:
  - The Secret Forces: `Nekroz of Clausolas`, `Nekroz of Brionac`, `Nekroz of Trishula`,
    `Nekroz of Unicore`, `Nekroz of Valkyrus`, `Nekroz Mirror`, `Nekroz Kaleidoscope`,
    `Shurit, Strategist of the Nekroz`.
    Brionac, Trishula and Valkyrus were heavily short printed.
  - Secrets of Eternity (the early wave): `Nekroz of Gungnir`, `Nekroz Cycle`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Djinn Releaser of Rituals` dumped by `Lavalval Chain`,
    reached through `Manju of the Ten Thousand Hands` or `Senju of the Thousand Hands`
    with Unicore: the one-sided "Djinn lock".
  - `Preparation of Rites` and `Herald of the Arc Light` (for Kaleidoscope).
  - Answers to the opposing Djinn lock: `D.D. Warrior Lady` (YCS Bochum),
    `Buster Blader` and `Book of Eclipse` (YCS Columbus onward).
  - `Solemn Scolding` (Central America WCQ).
  - `Performage Trick Clown` and the Performage engine (YCS Rimini).
  - At Worlds: `Exa, Enforcer of the Nekroz`, `Great Sorcerer of the Nekroz` and the Hands.
- Results: won YCS Tacoma (Feb), dominated the YCS Prague top cut (Mar),
  won YCS Chicago with over half
  the top cut (Apr), YCS Bogota and YCS Bochum (Apr), YCS Columbus (May),
  the Oceania, Central America and South American WCQs (Jun to Jul),
  second place at Worlds and won YCS Rimini (Aug).
- Ended by: the Apr 1st list (Brionac semi-limited, Preparation of Rites limited)
  did little; the Jul 16th list banned Djinn and Lavalval Chain and limited Shurit;
  the Nov 9th list banned Shurit and limited Brionac, Unicore
  and `Reinforcement of the Army`, a killing blow.

## Shaddoll

- Top period: Jan, then Apr to Jul (about 5 months).
  The Jan 1st list targeted it as a top deck (`Super Polymerization` banned);
  it fell from grace with no new support in Secrets of Eternity,
  then came back from Apr.
- New strong support: `El Shaddoll Anoyatyllis` (Crossed Souls), against Nekroz.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Crush Card Virus` after its errata (Apr list), against Nekroz.
  - `Star Seraph Scepter` and `Star Seraph Sovereignty` (World Superstars):
    draws, LIGHTs for `El Shaddoll Construct`,
    and rank four access to `Evilswarm Ouroboros` for a turn one hand rip.
  - `Peropero Cerperus` and `Mathematician` to reach `El Shaddoll Shekhinaga`
    (European WCQ).
- Results: resurgence at YCS Chicago (Apr), the primary changed deck at YCS Bogota
  and a big rise at YCS Bochum (Apr), won the European WCQ (Jul),
  its strongest performance of the year.
- Ended by: OCG hits that made it suboptimal at Worlds (Aug),
  then the Nov 9th list (Construct banned, `El Shaddoll Fusion` and Mathematician limited).

## Infernoid

- Top period: Jun to Oct (about 5 months).
- New archetype and support:
  - Secrets of Eternity: `Infernoid Antra`, `Infernoid Harmadik`, `Infernoid Patrulea`,
    `Infernoid Piaty`, `Infernoid Seitsemas`, `Infernoid Attondel`, `Infernoid Onuncu`,
    `Void Seer`. Rogue: the first swarm cost too many resources.
  - Crossed Souls: `Infernoid Devyaty`, `Infernoid Pirmais`, `Infernoid Sjette`.
  - Clash of Rebellions: `Infernoid Decatron` (the first normal summonable one),
    `Infernoid Tierra`, `Void Imagination`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - A small Lightsworn engine for early mills,
    with `Lyla, Lightsworn Sorceress` and `Ehren, Lightsworn Monk`.
  - `Minerva, the Exalted Lightsworn` (YCS Toronto prize card).
  - `PSY-Framelord Omega` (High-Speed Riders): hand rip and level removal.
- Results: first premier top in the Central America WCQ top 32 (Jun),
  adapted to Decatron at YCS Toronto (Aug),
  won YCS Dallas undefeated at 16-0-0 (Oct).

## Qliphort

- Top period: Jan to Apr (about 4 months).
- New strong support (Secrets of Eternity): `Qliphort Monolith`, `Qliphort Stealth`,
  `Re-qliate`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Soul Transition`, a Pot of Greed for Qliphort.
  - `Saqlifice`: a normal summoned Qli with it was one of the few ways
    out of the Djinn lock (YCS Tacoma).
  - `Lose 1 Turn` (Crossed Souls), clearly targeted for it.
- Results: topping lists at YCS Charleston (Jan), did fairly well at YCS Tacoma
  (Feb), adapted to Re-qliate at YCS Chicago (Apr).
- Ended by: the Apr 1st list (Saqlifice and `Skill Drain` limited,
  `Qliphort Scout` semi-limited),
  then the Nov 9th list (`Apoqliphort Towers` banned, Scout limited).

## Kozmo

- Top period: Aug to Nov (about 4 months).
- New archetype and support:
  - Clash of Rebellions: `Kozmo Farmgirl`, `Kozmo Goodwitch`, `Kozmo Sliprider`,
    `Kozmo Forerunner`, `Kozmotown`. Kozmotown loops with Sliprider for any card.
  - Dimension of Chaos: `Kozmo Strawman`, `Kozmoll Wickedwitch`, `Kozmo Dark Destroyer`,
    which raised it from tier three to tier one.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Emergency Teleport` for Farmgirl.
  - `Reasoning`: almost every ship and pilot has a different level.
- Results: top cut on its first outing at YCS Toronto (Aug),
  won YCS San Jose with the main slice of the top cut (Nov),
  9 of the top 16 and the win at YCS Sao Paulo (Nov).
  The Kaiju (`Kumongous, the Sticky String Kaiju`, `Gameciel, the Sea Turtle Kaiju`)
  were the main answer to Forerunner and Dark Destroyer.

## HERO

- Top period: Aug (about 1 month), in the table for its World Championship top 8.
- New strong support (HERO Strike): `Elemental HERO Shadow Mist`, `Mask Change II`,
  `Masked HERO Dark Law`, `Masked HERO Koga`, `Masked HERO Divine Wind`.
- Results: mostly a Dark Law side deck tech in other DARK decks;
  one list in the North American WCQ top 64 (Jun),
  top eight at Worlds with `Mystic Tomato` (Aug).

## Honorable mentions

Playable with strong new cards, but never won a major event in 2015
nor made the top cut at two or more.

### Ritual Beast

- New archetype (The Secret Forces): `Ritual Beast Tamer Lara`, `Ritual Beast Tamer Elder`,
  `Spiritual Beast Apelio`, `Spiritual Beast Pettlephin`, `Spiritual Beast Cannahawk`,
  `Ritual Beast Ulti-Cannahawk`, `Ritual Beast Steeds`, `Ritual Beast's Bond`;
  later `Ritual Beast Tamer Zeframpilica` and `Ritual Beast Ulti-Gaiapelio` (Crossed Souls).
- Results: tier three, first premier event top at YCS Chicago (Apr),
  with Steeds popping the Djinn lock without targeting.
- Ended by: the Nov 9th list (Ulti-Cannahawk limited).

### Volcanic

- New strong support: `Blaze Accelerator Reload` (Secrets of Eternity),
  with `Volcanic Scattershot`, `Royal Firestorm Guards` and `Magic Planter`.
- Results: undefeated in the Swiss rounds at YCS Charleston (Jan),
  then a one-of in top cuts; Volcanic HERO was a rogue take at YCS Bochum (Apr).

### Yang Zing

- New strong support: `Zefraxi, Treasure of the Yang Zing`
  and `Zefraniu, Secret of the Yang Zing` (Crossed Souls).
- Results: first ever premier top at YCS Toronto (Aug).

### Mecha Phantom Beast

- New strong support: `Mecha Phantom Beast Jaculuslan` (World Superstars), a turn one hand rip.
- Results: first ever top cut as its own deck at YCS Bochum (Apr), off surprise factor.

### Clown Blade (Performage and Heroic)

- New strong support: the Performage engine (Clash of Rebellions)
  with `Heroic Challenger - Assault Halberd` and `Heroic Challenger - Thousand Blades`
  (World Superstars), plus `Gigobyte` and `King of the Feral Imps`.
- Results: top cut at YCS Dallas (Oct).

### Majespecter

- New archetype (Dimension of Chaos): `Majespecter Cat - Nekomata`,
  `Majespecter Raccoon - Bunbuku`, `Majespecter Unicorn - Kirin` and their spells and traps.
- Results: top 16 at YCS San Jose (Nov), with `Moon Mirror Shield`.

### Performapal Performage ("Pepe")

- New strong support: `Luster Pendulum, the Dracoslayer`,
  `Ignister Prominence, the Blasting Dracoslayer` (Clash of Rebellions),
  `Performage Plushfire`, `Majester Paladin, the Ascending Dracoslayer` (Dimension of Chaos),
  `Performapal Skullcrobat Joker`, `Sky Iris`, `Pendulum Call` (Master of Pendulum).
- Results: first major event top at YCS Sao Paulo (Nov).
  Pepe Magicians rose off Master of Pendulum,
  but YCS Lille, the last YCS of the year, was cancelled (Dec).
