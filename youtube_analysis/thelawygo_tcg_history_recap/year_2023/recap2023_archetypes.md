World Championship 2023 winner: Dragon Link, Paulie Aronson
(United States, the first American world champion; held in Tokyo on Aug 6th,
between Duelist Nexus and Legendary Duelists: Soulburning Volcano; the captions say "Paul Aronson").
Spelling checked against Konami's press release and Road of the King,
which lists him as Paul Stephen Aronson, playing Bystial Dragon Link.
Worlds used the combined TCG and OCG card pool and ban list, with a more limited deck selection.

The video calls Tearlaments the tier zero mega threat at the start of the year
("exiting 2022 nothing had changed"), so it stayed tier zero into Jan 2023.
The video does not say when that began (Nov 2022 is not mentioned),
and the Feb 13th ban list ended it.

## Kashtira

- Top period: Feb to Sep (about 8 months), the best deck of the format.
  Taking 14 of the top 64 at the Central America WCQ and 15 top spots at the European WCQ,
  and the most represented deck at nearly every event until the Sep 25th ban list.
- New archetype support (Photon Hypernova): `Kashtira Riseheart`, `Scareclaw Kashtira`,
  `Tearlaments Kashtira`, `Kashtira Overlap`, `Kashtiratheosis`, `Pressured Planet Wraitsoth`,
  `Kashtira Big Bang` and the rank seven `Kashtira Arise-Heart`,
  which with `Kashtira Unicorn` into `Kashtiratheosis` into `Kashtira Fenrir` made a one card setup.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Kashtira Shangri-Ira` plus Arise-Heart locked the opponent out before they had a turn.
  - Tearlaments Kashtira merged the two strategies and milled on turn zero.
  - Slimmed builds (8 monsters and 10 spells and traps) left room for hand traps
    like `PSY-Framegear Gamma` and `Ghost Mourner & Moonlit Chill`.
  - `Book of Moon` and `Forbidden Lance` protected the lockout against its counters.
- Results: won YCS Lyon (Chris LeBlanc, fifth YCS win), YCS Bogota,
  YCS Los Angeles (Paulie Aronson),
  YCS Santiago, YCS Philadelphia (Dennis Nus), the South American, Oceania and North American WCQs
  (Jeremy Mitchell), and took the most top cut spots at the European WCQ.
- Ended by: the Sep 25th ban list banned `Kashtira Arise-Heart`
  (limited in Jun, with `Kashtira Unicorn` semi-limited);
  it only made a stun style top cut at the last YCS in Barcelona.

## Tearlaments

- Top period: Jan to Feb, then Sep to Nov (about 5 months in total).
  Tier zero until the Feb 13th ban list,
  then back to one of the top decks after Arise-Heart was banned,
  still making a case for itself at YCS Richmond in Nov.
- New strong support: `Tearlaments Kashtira` (Photon Hypernova), the same card that tied it
  to Kashtira.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Dimension Shifter` and `Gravekeeper's Inscription` as counters,
  `Evenly Matched` in the Floowandereeze matchup.
- Results: Jesse Cotton won YCS Sydney (fourth YCS win),
  Chris LeBlanc won YCS Lyon (fifth), three tops at YCS Indianapolis,
  and it was the most represented deck again at YCS Dortmund (Sep).
- Ended by: the Feb 13th ban list (`Tearlaments Kitkallos` banned,
  most of its 2022 monsters limited); the Sep return was not stopped before the year ended.

## Spright

- Top period: Feb to Jun (about 5 months), not the best deck but always present.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Spright Elf` (banned Feb 13th) was the enabler; afterwards it adapted with Live Twin,
    Runick and Kashtira splashes.
  - `Gishki Grimness` and `Gishki Abyss` as level two rogue starters.
  - The Adventure package (Floowandereeze) with `Assault Synchron` and `Number 29: Mannequin Cat`.
  - `Gigantic Spright` into `Rex, Freight Fur Hire`.
- Results: won YCS Lima (Jean Guero, Adventure Spright), took 10 slots in the top 64 at YCS London
  (Samir Bakkar won), and Carlos Mot won the Central America WCQ with Live Twin Runick Spright.
- Ended by: the Jun 5th ban list hit the deck and Kashtira alike,
  after which it faded behind Dragon Link and Rikka Sunavalon.

## Labrynth

- Top period: Feb to May, then Nov (about 5 months in total).
  Already a meta threat before the Feb structure deck,
  back with a resurgence at Team YCS Las Vegas, then a quiet stretch from Jun to Oct.
- New strong support: `Big Welcome Labrynth` (Photon Hypernova)
  and `Arias the Labrynth Butler` (Age of Overlord).
- Results: three of the top 64 at YCS London,
  then six of the top 64 and a top eight at YCS Philadelphia,
  where it became Furniture Labrynth;
  one of the decks putting up numbers at YCS Richmond.

## Purrely

- Top period: May to Jun, then Sep to Nov (about 5 months in total).
- New archetype (Amazing Defenders): `Purrely`, `Epurrely Happiness`, `Epurrely Noir`,
  `Expurrely Noir`,
  `My Friend Purrely`; later `Purrelyly` and `Purrely Sleepy Memory` (Cyberstorm Access),
  `Epurrely Noir` and `Purrely Sharely!?` (Duelist Nexus).
- Key non-archetype cards: `Nibiru, the Primal Being` and `Droll & Lock Bird`
  as the popular hand traps at YCS Indianapolis.
- Results: four players in the top cut at YCS Philadelphia (none past top 32),
  four tops at the Oceania Championships (one second place),
  comfortable in the meta at YCS Vancouver and still putting up numbers at YCS Richmond.
- Ended by: `Purrely Delicious Memory` limited on Jun 5th ended the first run;
  `Epurrely Noir` brought it back in the autumn.

## Dragon Link

- Top period: Jun to Oct (about 5 months), a mid power deck that never died.
- New strong support:
  - Bystial cards (the video says "Bisal"):
    `Bystial Dis Pater` (Cyberstorm Access) recurred banished dragons.
  - Structure Deck: The Crimson King: `Vision Resonator` and `Crimson Gaia` as an engine,
    reaching `Bystial Dis Pater` easily.
  - Age of Overlord: the Horus cards for `The Zombie Vampire` and
    `Number 38: Hope Harbinger Dragon Titanic Galaxy`.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Baronne de Fleur`, `Accel Synchro Stardust Dragon`,
  `Revolution Synchron` and `Crimson Dragon` (Duelist Nexus) for synchro extension.
- Results: topped the European WCQ, won the World Championship 2023 (Aug 6th, Paulie Aronson),
  rose in popularity at YCS Vancouver, and kept performing at YCS Dortmund
  despite `Bystial Magnamhut` and `Chaos Space` being limited on Sep 25th.
- Ended by: nothing in 2023; Little Knight and Age of Overlord decks passed it in Oct.

## Branded

- Top period: Feb to May (about 4 months).
- Key non-archetype cards: `Gimmick Puppet Nightmare`, `Branded Expulsion` (banned Jun 5th),
  `Granguignol the Dusk Dragon`, and the Chimera line (`Chimera the King of Phantom Beasts`,
  `Berfomet the Mythical King of Phantom Beasts`).
- Results: second largest top cut share at Team YCS Las Vegas (after the Feb ban list),
  three of the top 64 at YCS London, top 32 at YCS Philadelphia.
- Ended by: `Branded Expulsion` banned on Jun 5th.

## Mathmech

- Top period: Mar to Jun (about 4 months).
- Key non-archetype cards: `Firewall Dragon Darkfluid - Neo Tempest Terahertz` as a finisher
  (Maze of Memories), and the Spirit engine to reach `Albion the Sanctifire Dragon` quickly.
- Results: four of the top 64 at YCS London,
  a couple of surprise tops at the South American WCQ.
- Ended by: `Mathmech Circular`, the one card combo starter, limited on Jun 5th.

## Unchained

- Top period: Sep to Dec (about 4 months), a breakout after Duelist Nexus.
- New strong support (Duelist Nexus): `Unchained Soul of Sharvara`, `Unchained Soul of Shyama`,
  `Unchained Soul Lord of Yama`.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Tour Guide From the Underworld` as a full Unchained line,
  `D/D/D Vice King Requiem` to pop its own cards, and a "dark contract" tech for the combo line.
- Results: took nine top spots and won YCS Vancouver (Jesse Cotton, his fifth YCS win),
  right behind Kashtira at YCS Cancun, most represented at YCS Richmond (Joseph Bellor won).
- Ended by: not ended in 2023; it shared the top with Tearlaments, Rescue-ACE and Labrynth.

## Floowandereeze

- Top period: Jan, Jul and Sep (about 3 months), never the most played
  but a deck that kept bouncing in and out of top cuts, usually in a single slot.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Dimension Shifter` and `Evenly Matched` against Tearlaments;
  its matchup into Kashtira was solid because the Arise-Heart macro effect was not an issue.
- Results: second place at YCS Sydney, a strong performance at the European WCQ,
  and Andre Curioso won YCS Cancun while the meta centralized around Kashtira and Unchained.
- Ended by: nothing in 2023.

## Runick

- Top period: Jan to Mar (about 3 months).
- Key non-archetype cards: Naturia synchros backed by the Runick spells (Naturia Runick),
  a mix with Plunder Patroll for control and resources.
- Results: a couple of pure Runick breakouts in the YCS Sydney top cut
  focused on locking out Tearlaments, Naturia Runick rising rapidly at YCS Lima,
  and a third place at YCS London.
- Ended by: `Naturia Sacred Tree` limited on Jun 5th,
  which obliterated Naturia Runick out of the meta.

## Infernoble

- Top period: Sep to Nov (about 3 months).
- New strong support (Duelist Nexus): `Infernoble Knight Ricciardetto`, `Infernoble Knight Turpin`,
  `Angelica, Princess of Noble Arms`, `Noble Arms Museum`; `Angelica's Angelic Ring` later.
- Results: top eight at YCS Vancouver, tops at YCS Santa Cruz and YCS Richmond.

## Rescue-ACE

- Top period: Oct to Dec (about 3 months), after being a rogue deck since Jan.
- New strong support:
  - Duelist Nexus: `Rescue-ACE Preventer`
    and `EMERGENCY!` ironed out its bricks (YCS Vancouver top).
  - Age of Overlord: `Diabellstar the Black Witch`, `WANTED: Seeker of Sinful Spoils`
    and Sinful Spoils
    Snake-Eye cards gave any opening a path to `Rescue-ACE Hydrant` on turn one.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Rescue-ACE Air Lifter` into `EMERGENCY!`
  to summon `Rescue-ACE Turbulence`
  and set four spells and traps.
- Results: 12 top spots and first place at YCS Indianapolis (Steven Santoli),
  still a contender at YCS Richmond.
- Ended by: nothing in 2023.

## Swordsoul

- Top period: Jan and Oct (about 2 months).
- Key non-archetype cards: the Bystial package, mixing level six bodies with Swordsoul tokens
  to hit level 10 (`Baronne de Fleur`, `Swordsoul Supreme Sovereign - Chengying`
  and `Swordsoul Sinister Sovereign - Qixing Longyuan`).
- Results: in the YCS Sydney top cut, then two top spots at YCS Santa Cruz,
  one standard Tenyi Swordsoul and one Superheavy Samurai Tenyi Swordsoul.

## Rikka Sunavalon

- Top period: Jul and Oct (about 2 months).
- Results: won the European WCQ (Jessica Robinson) and YCS Dortmund (Julius Schwar Scoth).

## Superheavy Samurai

- Top period: May (about 1 month),
  hit by the Jun 5th ban list before it had a real chance to shine.
- New strong support (Cyberstorm Access): `Superheavy Samurai Prodigy Wakaushi`,
  `Superheavy Samurai Monk Big Benkei`, `Superheavy Samurai Scarecrow`.
- Results: three of the known 11 slots at YCS Santiago,
  then 12 top slots and a top eight at YCS Philadelphia.
- Ended by: `Cyber-Stein` and `Superheavy Samurai Scarecrow` banned on Jun 5th
  because they enabled one card wins on turn one.

## Vanquish Soul

- Top period: Jun (about 1 month).
- New archetype (Wild Survivors): `Vanquish Soul Razen`, `Vanquish Soul Pantera`,
  `Vanquish Soul Heavy Borger`, `Vanquish Soul Dr. Mad Love`, `Vanquish Soul Pluton HG`.
- Results: three in the top 64 and a top eight at the Central America WCQ,
  six of the known 23 top spots and a top eight at the South American WCQ.
- Held back by: no `Maxx "C"` in the TCG; ultra rare slots made the core cost about $300.

## Mikanko

- Top period: Oct (about 1 month), YCS Santa Cruz was a week after Age of Overlord.
- New archetype (Amazing Defenders): `Ohime the Manifested Mikanko`, `Ha-Re the Sword Mikanko`,
  `Ni-Ni the Mirror Mikanko`; `Hu-Li the Jewel Mikanko` and `Mikanko Dance - Mayowashidori` later.
- Results: Diabellstar Mikanko won YCS Santa Cruz (Michael Verissimo da Silva)
  using `Ken the Warrior Dragon` and `Gen the Diamond Tiger`.

## Bystial Runick

- Top period: Dec (about 1 month), a deck no one was expecting.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  `Guiding Quem, the Virtuous` for access to `Blazing Cartesia, the Virtuous`,
  `The Bystial Aluber` as a searchable level four tuner,
  and Duality to reach different extra deck monsters off the levels and attributes in the deck.
- Results: Joshua Schmidt won YCS Barcelona, his fourth YCS win.

## Honorable mentions

Playable with strong new cards, but never won a major event in 2023
nor made the top cut at two or more.

### Fire King

- New strong support (Structure Deck: Fire Kings):
  `Legendary Fire King Ponix`, `Sacred Fire King Garunix`,
  `Garunix Eternity, Hyang of the Fire Kings`.
- Results: four top spots at YCS Barcelona, all with the Diabellstar engine.

### Centur-Ion

- New archetype (Valiant Smashers): `Centur-Ion Primera`, `Centur-Ion Legatia`,
  `Stand Up Centur-Ion!`, `Centur-Ion True Awakening`.
- Results: four top spots at YCS Barcelona (Horus, Bystial and Superheavy Samurai variants).

### Mannadium

- New archetype (Cyberstorm Access): `Mannadium Riumheart`, `Mannadium Prime-Heart`,
  `Mannadium Abscission`, `Peaceful Planet Calarium`; `Visas Samsara` (Age of Overlord).
- Results: two tops at YCS Indianapolis, a Scareclaw version at YCS Cancun.

### Newer archetypes to watch

- Gold Pride (Photon Hypernova, Cyberstorm Access): `Gold Pride - Leon`,
  `Gold Pride - Roller Baller`,
  two top spots at YCS Philadelphia with the P.U.N.K. engine.
- Traptrix (Structure Deck: Beware of Traptrix):
  `Traptrix Pudica`, `Traptrix Pinguicula`, a top at YCS Lima.
- Salamangreat (Legendary Duelists: Soulburning Volcano):
  `Salamangreat Burst Gryphon`, no meta movement.
