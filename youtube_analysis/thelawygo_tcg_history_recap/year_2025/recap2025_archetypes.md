Summary of [recap2025_transcript.md](recap2025_transcript.md)

Top archetypes are ordered by how long they stayed a top deck, longest first.

World Championship 2025 winner: K9 Vanquish Soul, Julien Leo Kehon (United States).
The video's captions have "Julian Leo Kahon" (heard as); the spelling was checked on
[roadoftheking.com](https://roadoftheking.com/yu-gi-oh-world-championship-2025/),
which also lists the final opponent as Tom Kleinegraeber (Germany) on Lunalight.
Worlds was held in Paris on Aug 30th and 31st,
between YCS Vancouver and the Sep 15th ban list.
The video says it was the third year in a row that the United States won,
and that several TCG decks were unhit or mirrored in the OCG,
but Mitsurugi was missing as a TCG exclusive.
The top eight decks named were K9, Yummy, Lunalight, Sky Striker, Crystron,
and Maliss (hit much harder in the OCG).

2025 sets in release order, for reference:
Supreme Darkness (Jan 24th), Structure Deck: Blue-Eyes White Destiny (Feb 14th),
Maze of the Master (Mar 14th), Alliance Insight (May 2nd),
Battles of Legend: Monster Mayhem (Jun 13th, but Aug 28th in Europe, the Middle East,
Africa and Oceania), Duelist's Advance (Jul 4th), Justice Hunters (Aug 1st),
Doom of Dimensions (Sep 26th), The Chronicles Deck: The Fallen & The Virtuous (Oct 24th),
Phantom Revenge (Dec 5th, and Dec 19th in the Americas).

Ban lists: Apr 7th, Sep 15th, and an emergency list on Oct 27th
(the first emergency list since 2016 or 2018, depending on who you ask).
Genesis format (a point system, no Links or Pendulums) was announced about nine days after
the Sep list.

## Ryzeal

- Top period: Jan to Sep (about 9 months),
  the top performer from late 2024 through Apr,
  then the engine half of Mitsurugi Ryzeal and Ryzeal heraldic beast builds.
- New support:
  - Alliance Insight: `Star Ryzeal`, `Ryzeal Mass Driver`,
    which made `Ryzeal Cross` easier to reach without risking `Ryzeal Duo Drive`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Eclipse Twins` (Supreme Darkness): a rank four enabler that keeps `Ryzeal Detonator` alive
    and revives it after use.
  - The Fiendsmith engine, with `A Bao A Qu, the Lightless Shadow` (Supreme Darkness)
    as a link four boss.
  - `King of the Feral Imps` as the search and bridge into Mitsurugi.
  - `Seventh Tachyon` (Maze of the Master) searching `Ice Ryzeal` or `Ext Ryzeal` through
    `Number 104: Masquerade`, and `Seventh Ascension` to find it for free.
  - `Dodododo Warrior` (Duelist's Advance) as a one card no normal rank four starter,
    and `Onomatokage` (Doom of Dimensions).
  - `Infernal Flame Banshee` and `Gallant Granite` fetched floodgates late in the year.
- Results: won YCS Orlando (Tavon Crowley), Team YCS San Salvador (Cameron Neil),
  Team YCS Las Vegas (Jesse Cotton's team, his seventh YCS win), YCS Sydney (Frederick Camel)
  and the Oceania WCQ (Andy Leu, heraldic beast build), top four at YCS Anaheim.
- Ended by: the Apr 7th list (`Ryzeal Detonator` and `Bonfire` limited,
  `Ext Ryzeal`, `Ice Ryzeal` and `Sword Ryzeal` semi-limited) weakened it;
  the Sep 15th list limited `Ext Ryzeal` and banned `King of the Feral Imps`.

## Maliss

- Top period: Jan to Sep (about 9 months), with a revival in Europe up to Nov.
- New support:
  - Alliance Insight: `Maliss <P> March Hare` (an in-archetype Bystial) and
    `Maliss in the Mirror`, which gave back everything the first wave lacked.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Backup @Ignister`, `Wizard @Ignister` and `Allied Code Talker @Ignister`
    (Alliance Insight), because the Maliss link threes are 2300 attack Cyberse.
  - After the Apr list the Sky Striker package came in:
    `Sky Striker Mobilize - Engage!`, `Sky Striker Mecha - Hornet Drones`,
    `Sky Striker Ace - Kagari`.
  - `Splash Mage` as the link climbing tool.
- Results: won the South American WCQ (Felipe Zapda) and the Central America WCQ
  (Alexander Hernandez), seven spots and most represented at YCS Mexico City,
  most represented in the top 32 at YCS Providence, top four at YCS Bologna.
- Ended by: the Apr 7th list (`Knightmare Gryphon` and `Link Decoder` banned,
  `Maliss <P> Dormouse`, `Maliss <P> White Rabbit` and `Maliss in Underground` semi-limited)
  and the Sep 15th list (`Splash Mage` banned, `Maliss <P> Dormouse` limited).

## Mitsurugi

- Top period: May to Nov (about 7 months), first top (second place) at YCS Orlando
  in Feb, breakout at YCS Providence.
- New archetype (Supreme Darkness, TCG exclusive): `Mitsurugi no Mikoto, Kusanagi`,
  `Mitsurugi no Mikoto, Saji`, `Ame no Murakumo no Mitsurugi`, `Futsu no Mitama no Mitsurugi`,
  `Mitsurugi Prayers`, `Mitsurugi Ritual`, `Mitsurugi Great Purification`.
- New strong support (Alliance Insight): `Mitsurugi no Mikoto, Aramasa`,
  `Mitsurugi no Miko, Wousu`, `Ame no Habakiri no Mitsurugi`, `Mitsurugi Mirror`,
  `Mitsurugi Magatama`, `Mitsurugi Sacred Boundary`, `Mitsurugi Tempest`.
  The video calls it the best second wave a TCG exclusive archetype ever got.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `King of the Feral Imps` and `Ryzeal Duo Drive` bridged it with Ryzeal.
  - `Gallant Granite`, `Fossil Dyna Pachycephalo` and `Barrier Statue of the Drought`
    as end of combo floodgates.
  - `Ritual Raven` and `Angry Burger` (Duelist's Advance) to make the blue boys reliably.
  - `Herald of the Arc Light` as the rank four bridge into Yummy
    after the King of the Feral Imps ban.
- Results: won YCS Providence (Charlie Futch), top performing deck at the South American WCQ,
  29 of the top 64 at the Oceania WCQ, then as Mitsurugi Yummy won YCS Lima (Trip),
  YCS Sao Paulo (Waqin Poyagi) and YCS Merida (James Marcoitz).
- Not ended in 2025: the Sep 15th list banned `King of the Feral Imps`,
  but the Yummy version kept winning.

## K9 Vanquish Soul

- Top period: Jul to Nov (about 5 months),
  with a dip in Oct after the Sep 15th list before Europe rebounded in Nov.
- New support: Duelist's Advance gave Vanquish Soul `Vanquish Soul Hollie Sue`,
  `Vanquish Soul Rocks` and `Vanquish Soul, Start!` (it won the European WCQ in Jul,
  taking 18 of the top 64, Lorenzo Muzelli).
- New archetype (Justice Hunters): `K9-17 Izuna`, `K9-66a Jokul`, `K9-66b Lantern`,
  `K9-ØØ Lupis`, `K9-LC Release Restraint`, `K9-X Forced Release`, `"A Case for K9"`,
  `K9-EW Special Release Experiment`, `K9-17 "Ripper"`, `K9-ØØ "Hound"`, `K9-X "Werewolf"`.
- New strong support (Doom of Dimensions): `K9-04 Noroi`, `K9-66X "Jacks"`.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Number 67: Pair-a-Dice Smasher` as the win condition from
  several level five monsters, `Vallon, the Super Psy Skyblaster`.
- Results: second place and most represented at YCS Vancouver, World Champion Julien Leo Kehon,
  won YCS Bologna (Pierre Burgles) and surged again at YCS Merida.
- Ended by: the Sep 15th list banned `Number 67: Pair-a-Dice Smasher` and limited
  `Vanquish Soul Razen` and `Vanquish Soul, Start!`;
  only one player in the YCS Lao top 64, but Europe recovered with a heavier K9 build.

## Yummy

- Top period: Aug to Nov (about 4 months).
- New archetype (Justice Hunters): `Cupsy☆Yummy`, `Cooky☆Yummy`, `Lollipo☆Yummy`,
  `Yummyusment☆Mignon`, `Yummyusment★Acroquey`, `Yummy☆Surprise`, `Yummy★Redemption`,
  `Yummy★Snatchy`, `Cupsy★Yummy Way`, `Cooky★Yummy Way`, `Lollipo★Yummy Way`.
- New strong support (Doom of Dimensions): `Marshmao☆Yummy`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Obedience Schooled` (reprinted in Justice Hunters) to summon one of each.
  - `Sky Striker Mecha - Hornet Drones` into `Sky Striker Ace - Kagari` as a full combo.
  - `Martial Metal Marcher` into `Herald of the Arc Light` for early insulation.
  - It never used effects from the hand or graveyard, which made the K9 matchup good.
- Results: won YCS Vancouver (Trift), YCS Lima (Trip, as Mitsurugi Yummy),
  YCS Sao Paulo and YCS Merida (Mitsurugi Yummy), top eight at Worlds.
- Ended by: not ended; the Sep 15th list limited `Obedience Schooled` and
  `Herald of the Arc Light`.

## Memento

- Top period: Feb to Apr (about 3 months), from a first top eight in Feb.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Goblin Biker Grand Breakout`, `Goblin Biker Big Gabonga`,
  `Goblin Biker Dugg Charger`, `Goblin Biker Grand Entrance`.
- Results: won YCS Houston (Pascal Menach) and YCS Mexico City (Renee Chapot).

## Crystron

- Top period: Aug to Oct (about 3 months) as Crystron K9,
  after a quiet first half of the year.
- New support (Supreme Darkness): `Crystron Tristaros`, `Crystron Sulfador`,
  `Crystron Inclusion`, `Crystron Cluster`, `Crystron Eleskeletus`,
  and `Clockwork Knight` to reach the one card combo from `Crystron Smiger`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Cherubini, Ebon Angel of the Burning Abyss` and `Scrap Recycler` to start the line.
  - `Infinitrack River Stormer` and `Chaotic Elements` in the K9 build,
    and `K9-04 Noroi` for instant rank fives.
  - `Artifact Dagda`, `Imperial Princess Quinquery` and `Artifact Mjollnir` made a summon lock.
- Results: top eight at YCS Sydney, top four at YCS Vancouver, Worlds top eight,
  won YCS Anaheim (Kamal Krooks Valdez, his sixth YCS win).
- Ended by: the Sep 15th list limited `Crystron Inclusion`;
  the emergency list banned `Artifact Mjollnir`.

## Dracotail

- Top period: Oct to Nov (about 2 months), as branded Dracotail.
- New archetype (Justice Hunters): `Dracotail Lukias`, `Dracotail Faimena`, `Dracotail Pan`,
  `Dracotail Urgula`, `Dracotail Mululu`, `Ketu Dracotail`, `Rahu Dracotail`,
  `Dracotail Horn`, `Dracotail Flame`, `Dracotail Arthalion`, `Dracotail Gulamel`.
- New strong support: `Dracotail Phryxul` (Doom of Dimensions),
  which makes `Branded Fusion` a one card starter;
  `The Fallen & The Virtuous` (The Chronicles Deck: The Fallen & The Virtuous)
  as an extra interaction point.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Branded Fusion`, `Blazing Cartesia, the Virtuous`,
  and a full set of hand traps.
- Results: top eight at YCS Vancouver, tied for top deck at YCS Sao Paulo,
  most represented in the top 32 at YCS Pittsburgh, won by Adita Darup.
- Ended by: not ended in 2025.

## Fire King

- Top period: Feb (about 1 month).
- Results: won YCS Birmingham (Dinka Boy, as heard) with a meta call against the Bystial decks.

## Mermail

- Top period: Mar (about 1 month).
- New support: Supreme Darkness `Abyssrhine, the Atlantean Spirit`, `Abyss-sting Triaina`,
  `Abyss-steam`, `Poseidra, the Storming Atlantean`.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Bahamut Shark`, `Toadally Awesome`, `Barrier Statue of the Torrent`.
- Results: won YCS Buenos Aires (Luca Daniel Garcia Gonzalez),
  placed at Team YCS San Salvador.
- Ended by: the Apr 7th list banned `Bahamut Shark` and `Abyss Dweller`.

## Evil Twin

- Top period: May (about 1 month).
- New support (Alliance Insight): `Live☆Twin Lil-la Sweet`, `Evil★Twin Ki-sikil Deal`.
- Results: won YCS Quito (Jose Bakara).

## Orcust

- Top period: Jul (about 1 month), from Monster Mayhem.
- New support: `Galatea-I, the Orcust Automaton`, `Enlilgirsu, the Orcust Mekk-Knight`.
- Results: won the North American WCQ (Wilfredo Flores).
- Not legal in the other WCQs because Monster Mayhem was delayed outside North America.

## Lunalight

- Top period: Aug (about 1 month), in the table for its World Championship second place.
- New strong support (Duelist's Advance): `Lunalight Gold Leo`, `Lunalight Silver Hound`,
  `Lunalight Masquerade`, `Lunalight Perfume Dancer`, `Lunalight Liger Dancer`.
- Results: top 32 at the North American WCQ, Worlds second place
  (the final opponent per Road of the King).

## Sky Striker

- Top period: Aug (about 1 month), in the table for its World Championship top 8.
- New strong support (Duelist's Advance): `Sky Striker Special Maneuver - Lemnisgate!`,
  `Sky Striker Ace = Zero`.
- Results: top eight at the European WCQ and at Worlds; mostly an engine in Maliss.

## Odion

- Top period: Oct (about 1 month).
- New archetype (Maze of the Master): `The Man with the Mark`, `Merciless Scorpion of Serket`,
  `Anubis the Last Judge`, `Treasures of the Kings`, `Apophis the Serpent`,
  `Divine Serpent Apophis`, `Divine Scorpion Beast of Serket`.
- Results: top 32 at YCS Houston, won YCS Lao (Diego Zilia, as Odion Primite).

## Honorable mentions

Playable with strong new cards, but never won a major event in 2025
nor made the top cut at two or more.

### Blue-Eyes

- New strong support (Structure Deck: Blue-Eyes White Destiny): `Maiden with Eyes of Blue`,
  `Wishes for Eyes of Blue`, `Roar of the Blue-Eyed Dragons`, `Majesty of the White Dragons`,
  `Spirit with Eyes of Blue`, `Blue-Eyes Ultimate Spirit Dragon`.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Primite Dragon Ether Beryl`, `Lightstorm Dragon`.
- Results: top four at YCS Sydney, then tier two or three fringe play.

### Gem-Knight

- New strong support (Monster Mayhem): `Gem-Knight Nepyrim`, `Gem-Knight Hollowcore`,
  `Gem-Knight Dispersion`, `Gem-Knight Master Diamond Dispersion`.
- Results: top four at the North American WCQ.

### Other archetypes to watch

- White Forest: `Elzette, Azamina of the White Forest`, `Saint Azamina`;
  top eight at YCS Birmingham.
- Regenesis (Alliance Insight): `Regenesis Archfiend`, top 32 at YCS Providence.
- Heraldic Beast (Alliance Insight): `Heraldic Beast Gryphon`; won the Oceania WCQ in a Ryzeal mix.
- Artmage (Duelist's Advance, the new lore archetype): `Medius the Pure`, `Artmage Diactorus`.
- Punk (Monster Mayhem): `Noh-P.U.N.K. Rising Scale`, `P.U.N.K. JAM FEVER!`.
- Evil HERO (Supreme Darkness): `Evil HERO Toxic Bubble`, `Dark Supremacy`;
  not worth the splash on its own.
- D/D/D (Doom of Dimensions): `D/D/D Zero Doom Queen Machinex`, a rogue level, complex deck.
- DoomZ and Radiant Typhoon (Doom of Dimensions): `DoomZ XII End - Drastrius`,
  `Radiant Typhoon Mandate`; strong in Genesis format but not in the regular TCG.
- Hecahands (Phantom Revenge): `Hecahands Gaigas`, `Hecahands Jauzah`.
- Enneacraft (Phantom Revenge): `Enneapolis`, a flip control deck, promising but brick prone.
- Kewl Tune (Phantom Revenge): `Kewl Tune Cue`, `Kewl Tune Remix`; the one with the most
  potential, held back by its all tuner extra deck.
