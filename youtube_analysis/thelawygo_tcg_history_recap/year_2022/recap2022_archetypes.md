# 2022 TCG: Top Archetypes

Summary of [recap2022_transcript.md](recap2022_transcript.md)
(TheLawYGO, The 2022 Yugioh TCG Recap).

Top archetypes are ordered by how long they stayed a top deck, longest first.
Months are approximate: the video dates set releases and ban lists exactly,
but most events only relative to them ("a week later").
The video covers up to the Dec 1st ban list, so Dec is counted as ongoing.

World Championship 2022: not held (cancelled 2020 to 2022 because of COVID-19;
Konami ran regional Territorial Championships instead). The video never mentions Worlds.
The biggest events it does cover:
YCS Charlotte (Apr, Prank-Kids), North American WCQ (Jul, Swordsoul Tenyi),
European Championship (Aug, Rikka Sunavalon), Oceanic Championship (Sep, Tearlaments)
and the Ishizu Tearlaments run of YCS Pasadena, Dortmund and San Jose (Nov).
Tearlaments with the Ishizu engine became the first tier zero deck since SPYRAL in 2017.

Ban lists: Feb 7th, May 17th, Oct 3rd, Dec 1st.

## Floowandereeze

- Top period: Jan to Dec (about 12 months),
  first as the Adventure engine splashed in front of nearly every deck,
  then as a standalone deck that kept topping until the end of the year.
- New archetype and engine:
  - The Grand Creators: the Adventure engine, `Wandering Gryphon Rider`,
    `Dracoback, the Rideable Dragon`, `Rite of Aramesir`,
    started by `Water Enchantress of the Temple`,
    `Foolish Burial` or `Emergency Teleport`.
  - Battle of Chaos: `Floowandereeze and the Advent of Adventure`,
    a search for any monster or the Field Spell, instantly a staple.
  - Dimension Force: `Illegal Knight`, a searchable interaction point.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - The Adventure engine in over half of the YCS Charlotte top 32.
  - A Simorgh engine (`Dark Simorgh` was banned in Feb) to generate extra Normal Summons.
  - `Droll & Lock Bird` and `Token Collector` in the Charlotte side deck.
  - `Dimension Shifter` and `Barrier Statue of the Stormwinds`,
    which gave Floowandereeze its strongest result of the year in Nov.
- Results: top cut at YCS Charlotte, the May 27th YCS,
  third at the North American WCQ (Pascal Monyat),
  second at YCS Minneapolis (Michael Thomas), a finalist at YCS Dortmund (Mario Aguiro),
  one of the eight at YCS San Jose.
- Not ended in 2022: the Dec 1st ban list banned `Mystic Mine` and limited
  `Herald of Orange Light`, aimed at helping Floowandereeze against Ishizu Tearlaments.

## Swordsoul Tenyi

- Top period: Jan to Jul (about 7 months),
  a constant in the top cut until Power of the Elements.
- New strong support: `Swordsoul Sinister Sovereign - Qixing Longyuan` (Battle of Chaos),
  a Level 10 Synchro that banishes and burns twice per turn,
  seen as a counter to the Adventure engine.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Super Polymerization` in the Charlotte side deck.
  - `Mystic Mine` and `Rivalry of Warlords` main decked in threes to counter out
    Noh-P.U.N.K., Branded Despia and others (North American WCQ).
- Results: top cut at YCS Charlotte and the May 27th YCS,
  won the North American WCQ (Han Sol Aguiro).
- Ended by: Spright and Tearlaments from Aug, which it had no answer for.

## Mystic Mine decks

- Top period: May to Nov (about 7 months), a non-archetype pillar of stun and floodgate builds.
- Key cards:
  - `Mystic Mine` (already banned in the OCG) with `Demise of the Land` to activate it
    on the opponent's turn.
  - `Vanity's Ruler`, `Rivalry of Warlords`, `Gozen Match`, `Ojama Trio` and `Ojama Duo`
    in the zero monster Mine Burn build.
  - Counters the community started main decking: `Harpie's Feather Duster` and `Cosmic Cyclone`.
- Results: won the May 27th YCS (Po Jiang, Sky Striker),
  both finalists of the North American WCQ (Han Sol Aguiro won),
  won YCS Rio (Xiao Pulau Lima, Mine Burn),
  and showed up in Altergeist (European Championship third).
  Three event wins in a couple of months sparked the loudest ban calls of the year.
- Ended by: the Dec 1st ban list banned `Mystic Mine`.

## Tearlaments

- Top period: Aug to Dec (about 5 months), tier zero from Nov.
- New archetype and support by set:
  - Power of the Elements: `Tearlaments Kitkallos`, `Tearlaments Kaleido-Heart`,
    `Tearlaments Merrli`, `Primeval Planet Perlereino`.
  - Darkwing Blast: `Tearlaments Rulkallos`, `Tearlaments Heartbeat`, `Tearlaments Scream`.
  - Magnificent Mavens: the Ishizu engine, `Agido the Ancient Sentinel`,
    `Kelbek the Ancient Vanguard`, `Mudora the Sword Oracle`, `Keldo the Sacred Protector`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - Ishizu monsters: mill five from both players, and return three to five cards to the Deck.
  - Danger! monsters, whose discard effects trigger Tearlaments (Danger! Tear,
    nicknamed "Casino Tear").
  - `Predaplant Dragostapelia`, `Time Thief Redoer` and `Spright Sprind` for extra Fusion turns.
  - Hand traps `D.D. Crow`, `Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring` and `Nibiru, the Primal Being`.
  - `Abyss Dweller`, resolved four times to win the YCS Pasadena finals.
  - `Mask Change II` into `Masked HERO Dark Law`, a mirror match tech.
- Results: 98 of the European Championship top 128 together with Spright,
  second at the European Championship (Zeo Mundry, Danger! Tear),
  took the whole Oceanic Championship top four (Dinos Pasovsky won),
  won YCS Minneapolis (Chris LeBlanc, Spright Tear), YCS Pasadena (Hani, 26 of the top 32),
  YCS Dortmund (Vincenzo Orofino) and YCS San Jose (Jesse Cotton).
- Hits: `Fairy Tail - Snow` banned on Oct 3rd, which did little.
  Not ended in 2022: the Dec 1st ban list buffed its counters instead
  (`Dimensional Fissure`, `Metaverse`, `Macro Cosmos`, `Herald of Orange Light` limited).

## Prank-Kids

- Top period: Feb to May (about 4 months), the biggest deck of the first half.
- Not new in 2022: already around, then boosted by The Grand Creators,
  since the Adventure engine made the Normal Summon effect unnecessary.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - The DPE engine: `Predaplant Verte Anaconda`, `Artifact Dagda`, `Artifact Scythe`
    and `Destiny HERO - Destroyer Phoenix Enforcer`.
  - The Adventure engine for omni negates.
- Results: 10 of the YCS Charlotte top 32 and the win (Elijah Green, Apr),
  still dominating at the Bogota and Guadalajara YCSs even with Branded on top.
- Ended by: the May 17th ban list
  (`Predaplant Verte Anaconda`, `Prank-Kids Meow-Meow-Mu`
  and `Mecha Phantom Beast Auroradon` banned).

## BASED

- Top period: Feb to May (about 4 months),
  a pile deck called "the deck to beat in the format" after Battle of Chaos.
- Built from `Illusion of Chaos`, `Magicians' Souls`, `Baronne de Fleur`,
  `Borreload Savage Dragon` and `Herald of the Arc Light` with the DPE engine.
- Results: first showings at YCS Charlotte as DPE Adventure Synchro,
  also used as the Punk engine's main home at the May 27th YCS.
- Ended by: faded after the May 17th ban list,
  which limited `Red Rose Dragon` and banned the DPE engine's `Predaplant Verte Anaconda`.

## Branded Despia

- Top period: Apr to Jul (about 4 months).
- New archetype and support by set:
  - Structure Deck: Albaz Strike: `Branded Fusion`, `Lubellion the Searing Dragon`,
    `Mirrorjade the Iceblade Dragon`, `Masquerade the Blazing Dragon`,
    `Branded in Red`, `Branded Opening`.
  - Ghosts From the Past: The 2nd Haunting: `Aluber the Jester of Despia` reprint.
  - Dimension Force: `Alba-Lenatus the Abyss Dragon`, `Branded Banishment`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Guardian Chimera` reached on the opponent's turn.
  - `Water Enchantress of the Temple` and the Adventure engine for extra insulation.
  - `Super Polymerization`-like effect from Banishment.
- Results: won the Bogota (Chris Lofton) and Guadalajara (Andre Torres) YCSs in Apr,
  stayed in the top cuts through the May 27th YCS and the North American WCQ.
- Later: `Fairy Tail - Snow` banned on Oct 3rd,
  and `The Bystial Lubellion` and `Bystial Baldrake` from Darkwing Blast fit into it.

## Noh-P.U.N.K.

- Top period: May to Aug (about 4 months).
- New archetype and support by set:
  - The Grand Creators: `Noh-P.U.N.K. Ze Amin`, `Noh-P.U.N.K. Foxy Tune`.
  - Dimension Force: `Noh-P.U.N.K. Deer Note`, which made the engine far stronger.
  - Power of the Elements: `P.U.N.K. JAM Extreme Session`, `P.U.N.K. JAM Dragon Drive`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Chaos Ruler, the Chaotic Magical Dragon` and `Crystron Halqifibrax` reached from Ze Amin alone.
  - `Emergency Teleport` to summon Ze Amin.
  - Used as an engine in BASED, Adventure and Therion decks.
- Results: the most represented deck at the North American WCQ
  (11 Punk Adventure and 6 Punk Therion lists in the top cut), but none made the finals.
- Ended by: `Chaos Ruler, the Chaotic Magical Dragon` and `Crystron Halqifibrax`
  banned on Oct 3rd, after Spright and Tearlaments had already pushed it down.

## Spright

- Top period: Aug to Oct (about 3 months).
- New archetype and support by set:
  - Power of the Elements: `Spright Elf`, ending on `Toadally Awesome`.
  - Darkwing Blast: `Spright Sprind`, `Spright Double Cross`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - Runick (Tactical Masters): `Hugin the Runick Wings` is Level 2,
    so every Quick-Play Runick spell starts Spright, forming Runick Spright.
  - `Garura, Wings of Resonant Life` and `Super Polymerization` as a tech against Spright openings.
  - `Nimble Angler`, `Nimble Beaver`, `Gigantic Spright`, `Knightmare Corruptor Iblee`,
    `Capshell`, `Wind-Up Kitten`, `Number 29: Mannequin Cat`.
- Results: lost its early lead to Tearlaments at the European Championship,
  Runick Spright won YCS Utrecht (Joshua Schmidt), Spright Tear won YCS Minneapolis
  (Chris LeBlanc), then was pushed back to a counter deck at YCS Pasadena.
- Ended by: `Ronintoadin` banned on Oct 3rd
  (it forced `Swap Frog` or a Live Twin or Paleozoic variant for `Toadally Awesome`),
  then Ishizu Tearlaments in Nov.

## Rikka

- Top period: Aug (about 1 month).
- New strong support (Power of the Elements period): `Rikka Konkon`,
  used by Rikka Sunavalon to Tribute the opponent's monsters.
- Results: won the European Championship (Marcus Patel), a surprise win
  that was the last Rikka top of the year.

## Exosister

- Top period: Sep (about 1 month).
- New strong support: `Exosisters Magnifica` (Dimension Force),
  `Exosister Martha` (Power of the Elements).
- Results: won YCS Niagara (Raymond Dye, defeating Jesse Cotton with `Dimension Shifter`),
  one of the only big Exosister finishes.

## Honorable mentions

Playable with strong new cards, but never won a major event in 2022
nor made the top cut at two or more.

### Mathmech

- New strong support (Power of the Elements): `Mathmech Circular`, `Mathmech Superfactorial`,
  `Primathmech Laplacian`, plus `Mathmech Nabla` and `The Arrival Cyberse @Ignister`.
- Results: second at YCS Rio (Marcus Lorenco).

### Therion and Scareclaw

- New archetype (Dimension Force): `Therion "King" Regulus`,
  with Scareclaw as the supplemental engine.
- Results: Punk Therion lists made the North American WCQ top cut,
  otherwise held back by needing a Machine to start.

### Bystial and Kashtira

- New archetype (Darkwing Blast): `Bystial Magnamhut`, `Bystial Druiswurm`, `Bystial Baldrake`,
  `The Bystial Lubellion` and `Kashtira Fenrir`, `Kashtira Unicorn`, `Kashtira Ogre`.
- Results: Bystial packages in every YCS Pasadena top four deck to counter Tearlaments;
  Kashtira only splashed, with `Kashtira Fenrir` over 70 dollars.

### Dinomorphia, Libromancer and Odd-Eyes

- New archetypes and support: `Dinomorphia Frenzy`, `Dinomorphia Rexterm` (Dimension Force),
  `Libromancer Doombroker`, `Libromancer Intervention`, `Odd-Eyes Pendulumgraph Dragon`,
  `Beyond the Pendulum`.
- Results: no meaningful tops, held back by Life Point costs and weak Pendulum.

### Other topping rogues

- Altergeist (third at the European Championship), Marincess (fourth at the North American WCQ),
  Madolche (YCS Dortmund top cut), Naturia with Vernusylph,
  and Ghoti (still missing its last piece).
- Drytron (top four at the May 27th YCS).
