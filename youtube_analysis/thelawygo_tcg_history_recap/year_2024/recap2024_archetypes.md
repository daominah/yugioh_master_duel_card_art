# 2024 TCG: Top Archetypes

Summary of [recap2024_transcript.md](recap2024_transcript.md)
(TheLawYGO, The 2024 Yugioh TCG Recap).

Top archetypes are ordered by how long they stayed a top deck, longest first.
Months are approximate: the video dates set releases and ban lists exactly,
but most events only relative to them ("two weeks later").
2024 had no structure decks and no duelist packs, so every new strategy came from a pack.

2024 sets in release order, for reference:
Maze of Millennia (January 19th), Phantom Nightmare (February 9th),
Legacy of Destruction (April 26th), Rarity Collection 2 (about late May),
Battles of Legend: Terminal Revenge (June 21st), The Infinite Forbidden (July 19th),
Dueling Mirrors (September 20th), Rage of the Abyss (October 11th),
Quarter Century Bonanza (November 8th), Crossover Breakers (December 6th).

Ban lists: January 1st, April 15th, September 2nd, December 8th.

World Championship 2024 winner: Fiendsmith Yubel,
Ruben Andres Penaranda (United States, name as heard).
It was the second World Championship in a row won by a player from the United States.
Fiendsmith was the main face of the event: `Fiendsmith's Lacrima` was still legal because the
September 2nd ban list came out a week before, with Tenpai Dragon and Ritual Beast sprinkled in.

## Snake-Eye

- Top period: February to December (about 11 months), the only tier one deck from February to
  April, a comfortable tier one through the Fiendsmith era,
  dropped for about a month after the September 2nd ban list,
  then back on top from October.
- New archetype support by set:
  - Phantom Nightmare: `Snake-Eyes Poplar` (the card that revolutionized the deck),
    `Promethean Princess, Bestower of Flames` as the fire link that lets Flamberge be reused.
  - Legacy of Destruction: `Snake-Eyes Diabellstar`, an extension piece for the pure build.
  - Rage of the Abyss: the Azamina cards `Azamina Ilia Silvia`, `Azamina Mu Rcielago`,
    `The Hallowed Azamina` and `Deception of the Sinful Spoils`,
    a new one card line with hand trap insulation after the September 2nd ban list.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Diabellstar the Black Witch` searching `Original Sinful Spoils - Snake-Eye`,
    the entry point for both the pure and the Fire King builds.
  - Fire King variant: `Legendary Fire King Ponix` reached through the searched
    `Original Sinful Spoils - Snake-Eye`; later `Fire King Courtier Ulcanix`
    and "Okex" (unresolved) from Rage of the Abyss gave it more starters.
  - `Bonfire` (Maze of Millennia) to search `Snake-Eye Ash`, because Ash is a level four pyro.
  - Synchro slots in early builds: `Jet Synchron`, `Formula Synchron`, `Borreload Savage Dragon`,
    `Baronne de Fleur`; insulation with `I:P Masquerena`, `Linkuriboh`
    and `Apollousa, Bow of the Goddess`.
  - Engines splashed in: Melodious (`Refrain the Melodious Songstress`, which gets to
    `Infernal Flame Banshee` for a pyro), Kashtira (`Kashtira Unicorn`),
    the Fiendsmith engine (`Moon of the Closed Heaven` made any three monsters a full line),
    and Millennium (`Wedju Temple` putting the Snake-Eyes in the spell trap zone).
  - Hand traps and `Mulcharmy Fuwalos` in the fall.
- Results: 39 of the top 48 at Team YCS San Jose and 40 of 48 at Team YCS Las Vegas
  (inflated by the team format), then won YCS Sydney (Jason Wang), YCS Medellin and
  YCS Guadalajara (Christian Urano), YCS Raleigh (Cameron Neil), YCS Indianapolis,
  the Central America and Oceania WCQs, YCS Sacramento (Jesse Cotton, Fiendsmith Snake-Eye),
  YCS Niagara Falls and YCS Guayaquil (Landon Oliver, Fire King variant).
- Hit by:
  - April 15th ban list (`Linkuriboh`, `Baronne de Fleur`, `Borrelsword Dragon`,
    `Borreload Savage Dragon`, `Summon Limit` banned): did not dethrone it.
  - September 2nd ban list (`Snake-Eye Ash` and `Snake-Eyes Poplar` limited,
    `Apollousa, Bow of the Goddess` and `Beatrice, Lady of the Eternal` banned): a month of
    weaker results.
  - December 8th ban list: `Original Sinful Spoils - Snake-Eye` banned,
    the one many players had wanted banned for half a year.

## Tenpai Dragon

- Top period: April to December (about 9 months), a tier two deck for most of it,
  never the best deck but the one that won a major WCQ.
- New archetype (Legacy of Destruction): `Tenpai Dragon Paidra`, `Tenpai Dragon Fadra`,
  `Tenpai Dragon Chundra`, `Sangenpai Bident Dragion`, `Sangenpai Transcendent Dragion`,
  `Sangen Summoning`, `Sangen Kaimen`.
  The Infinite Forbidden added `Tenpai Dragon Genroku` as a one card starter.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Trident Dragion`, a required 2009 card whose price skyrocketed,
    for the damage push (up to 37,400 damage from one card in the battle phase).
  - `Pot of Prosperity` (the deck needs only about 15 main deck slots,
    so 25 slots go to hand traps and consistency).
  - `Dimension Shifter`, `Mulcharmy Purulia` and board breakers
    `Lightning Storm`, `Dark Hole`, `Harpie's Feather Duster`, `Forbidden Droplet`.
- Results: topped YCS Rition Era (May, as the newcomer),
  rose to tier two with Yubel at the South America WCQ,
  and Esteban Re (name as heard) won the European WCQ (July).
- Hit by: September 2nd ban list (`Sangen Summoning` limited),
  December 8th ban list (`Tenpai Dragon Chundra` and `Sangen Kaimen` limited,
  its best starter from six copies down to two), but it kept topping.

## Yubel

- Top period: June to December (about 7 months), the dominant deck from late September to
  early October.
- New archetype (Phantom Nightmare): `Spirit of Yubel`, `Grinder Golem`, `Samsara D Lotus`,
  `Yubel - The Loving Defender Forever`, `Nightmare Pain`, `Mature Chronicle`, `Eternal Favorite`.
  Support: `Nightmare Throne` and `Gruesome Grave Squirmer` (Legacy of Destruction),
  `Phantom of Yubel` (Terminal Revenge), which gave it hand trap insulation.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - The Fiendsmith engine, helped by `Aerial Eater` (a TCG exclusive) so that Beatrice's
    ban hurt it less; the rank six slot became `D/D/D Wave High King Caesar`.
  - `Dark Beckoning Beast` and `Varudras, the Final Bringer of the End Times` for level 10 plays.
  - Kashtira package (`Kashtira Unicorn`) to rip a card from the opponent's extra deck.
- Results: third overall representation at the Central America WCQ (June),
  tier two at the South America WCQ and YCS Sacramento,
  won the World Championship (Ruben Andres Penaranda, Fiendsmith Yubel),
  YCS LY (Noel Hamill), Team YCS Santiago, YCS Cancun (Fiendsmith Yubel).
- Hit by: December 8th ban list (`Phantom of Yubel` limited, heavily hampering repeatability).

## Fiendsmith

- Top period: July to December (about 6 months), an engine, not a standalone deck:
  in 27 of the top 32 decks at YCS Sacramento.
- New archetype (The Infinite Forbidden): `Fiendsmith Engraver`, `Fiendsmith's Lacrima`,
  `Fiendsmith's Desirae`, `Fiendsmith Kyrie`, `Fiendsmith's Sequence`, `Fiendsmith's Tract`,
  `Fiendsmith's Requiem`, `Fiendsmith's Sanct`, `Fiendsmith in Paradise`.
  Later `Lacrima the Crimson Tears`, `Fiendsmith's Agnumday` (Rage of the Abyss).
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Fabled Lurrie` to reach Requiem; `Moon of the Closed Heaven` (OCG import)
    as a generic link two light fiend.
  - `Necroquip Princess` (TCG exclusive): a rank six off any two cards;
    `Aerial Eater` (TCG exclusive) for the mill setup after Beatrice.
  - `D/D/D Wave High King Caesar` as the omni-negate end of the line,
    `Beatrice, Lady of the Eternal` as the flexible rank six.
  - `Magical Musketeer Max`, `Magical Musketeer Starfire` and `Magical Musketeer Calamity`
    as light fiend starters (a pure Magical Musketeer Fiendsmith won YCS Barcelona).
- Results: the North American WCQ (July 19th release, the day before it),
  top deck at the European WCQ, 27 of the top 32 at YCS Sacramento,
  and the World Championship (Fiendsmith Yubel).
- Hit by: September 2nd ban list (`Fiendsmith's Lacrima` banned 52 days after its release,
  the fastest release to ban time ever, and `Beatrice, Lady of the Eternal` banned),
  yet the engine stayed in most top decks and Ryzeal could splash it in December.

## Voiceless Voice

- Top period: February to May (about 4 months), a tier three deck most of the time,
  a slower control deck that played low to the ground.
- New archetype (Phantom Nightmare): `Lo, the Prayers of the Voiceless Voice`,
  `Saffira, Dragon Queen of the Voiceless Voice`, `Sauravis, Dragon Sage of the Voiceless Voice`,
  `Skull Guardian, Protector of the Voiceless Voice`, `Prayers of the Voiceless Voice`,
  `Barrier of the Voiceless Voice`, `Radiance of the Voiceless Voice`.
- Key non-archetype cards: none highlighted, its draw was simply not being Snake-Eye.
- Results: topped Team YCS San Jose, overtook the Fire King variant in representation at
  YCS Guadalajara, tier two contender at YCS Rition Era.
- Ended by: Tenpai Dragon and Yubel taking the second best deck spot.

## Spright

Called "Sprite" in the video.

- Top period: June (about 1 month).
- Key non-archetype cards from Battles of Legend: Terminal Revenge:
  `Mirror Mage of the Ice Barrier`, which `Swap Frog` can dump,
  searching `Freezing Chains of the Ice Barrier` to revive it,
  for a one card access to `Toadally Awesome` or `Herald of the Arc Light`.
- Results: won the South America WCQ (Carlos Lur in Fontes, name as heard),
  the first premier event since Poplar's release that Snake-Eye did not win.
  Also topped the Central America WCQ.

## Ryzeal

- Top period: December (about 1 month), from the day of Crossover Breakers.
- New archetype (Crossover Breakers): `Sword Ryzeal`, `Node Ryzeal`, `Ice Ryzeal`,
  `Ext Ryzeal`, `Palm Ryzeal`, `Ryzeal Duo Drive`, `Ryzeal Detonator`, `Ryzeal Plugin`,
  `Ryzeal Cross`, `Ryzeal Plasma Hole`.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Bonfire` to search `Ice Ryzeal` (a level four pyro),
  the Fiendsmith engine (via Exaton, then Requiem) before the Ryzeal summon lock,
  `Dimension Shifter` and hand traps.
  `Ryzeal Detonator` shuts down continuous and field spells, hurting Snake-Eye and Yubel.
- Results: won YCS Anaheim (Trifunoski, name as heard), fulfilling a called shot
  to win a YCS by the end of the year.

## Maliss

- Top period: December (about 1 month).
- New archetype (Crossover Breakers): `Maliss <P> White Rabbit`, `Maliss <P> Chessy Cat`,
  `Maliss <P> Dormouse`, `Maliss <Q> Red Ransom`, `Maliss <Q> White Binder`,
  `Maliss <Q> Hearts Crypter`, `Maliss <C> MTP-07`, `Maliss <C> GWC-06`, `Maliss <C> TB-11`,
  `Maliss in Underground`, `Maliss in the Mirror`.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Firewall Dragon Darkfluid - Neo Tempest Terahertz` as the goal,
  `Haggard Lizardose` (TCG exclusive), `Link Decoder`, `Splash Mage`,
  `Decode Talker Heatsoul`, and `Dimension Shifter`, which suits a deck that wants its cards
  banished.
- Results: a strong debut at YCS Anaheim next to Ryzeal, with a core of only about 16 cards.

## Honorable mentions

Got strong new cards and were playable, but never became a top deck in 2024.

### Flame Swordsman

- New archetype (Maze of Millennia): `Fighting Flame Swordsman`, `Mirage Swordsman`,
  `Ultimate Flame Swordsman`, `Fighting Flame Dragon`, `Flame Swordsrealm`,
  `Salamandra, the Flying Flame Dragon`, `Salamandra Fusion`, `Flame Swordsdance`.
- Held back by: the Infernoble Knight support it wanted being banned just before its release
  (the key card heard as "solid").

### Earthbound

- New support (Maze of Millennia): `Earthbound Prisoner Ground Keeper`,
  `Earthbound Prisoner Stone Sweeper`, `Earthbound Prisoner Line Walker`,
  `Earthbound Prison`, `Harmonic Synchro Fusion`, `Earthbound Servant Geo Gremlin`.
- Not a deck; `Harmonic Synchro Fusion` was noted as a card for a future deck.

### Goblin Biker

- New archetype (Phantom Nightmare): `Goblin Biker Dugg Charger`, `Goblin Biker Boom Mach`,
  `Goblin Biker Big Gabonga`, `Goblin Bikers Gone Bonkers`.
- Held back by: doing nothing that the Punk and Gold Pride rank three decks did not already do.

### Melodious

- New support (Legacy of Destruction): `Refrain the Melodious Songstress`,
  `Bacha the Melodious Maestra`, `Flowering Etoile the Melodious Magnificat`,
  `Melodious Concerto`.
- Used as an engine for rank four and insulation (Snake-Eye, Centurion), not a deck.

### Ragnaraika

- New archetype (Legacy of Destruction): `Ragnaraika the Evil Seed`,
  `Ragnaraika Samurai Beetle`, `Ragnaraika Stag Sovereign`, `Ragnaraika Bloom`.
- Held back by: no payoff for the swarming; a plant link deck topped YCS LY once.

### Ritual Beast

- New support (Battles of Legend: Terminal Revenge): `Ritual Beast Tamer Lara`,
  `Ritual Beast Ulti-Nochiudrago`, `Ritual Beast Ulti-Reirautari`.
- Results: topped the Oceania WCQ and was sprinkled into the World Championship.

### Centurion

- New support: `Centur-Ion Gargoyle II`, `Centur-Ion Auxila`, `Wake Up Centur-Ion!`
  (Legacy of Destruction); `Centur-Ion Atrii` (The Infinite Forbidden);
  `Centur-Ion Primera Primus` (Rage of the Abyss).
- Results: topped the South America WCQ through the Melodious line.
  `Hot Red Dragon Archfiend King Calamity` (the Calamity lock) was banned September 2nd.

### White Forest

- New archetype (The Infinite Forbidden): `Astellar of the White Forest`,
  `Elzette of the White Forest`, `Diabell, Queen of the White Forest`.
- Results: first top at the European WCQ as a Runick mix-in, topped YCS LY.

### Gimmick Puppet

- New support (The Infinite Forbidden): `Gimmick Puppet Little Soldiers`,
  `Gimmick Puppet Rouge Doll`, `Mansion of the Dreadful Dolls`, `Service Puppet Play`.
- Results: topped the North American WCQ; a one card FTK that `Infinite Impermanence` stopped.
  `Number C40: Gimmick Puppet of Dark Strings` and `Gimmick Puppet Nightmare` were hit by the bans.

### Millennium

- New archetype (The Infinite Forbidden): `Millennium Revelation`,
  `The Unstoppable Exodia Incarnate`, `Sengenjin`, `Millennium Ankh`, `Wedju Temple`.
- Results: first top at YCS Niagara Falls in the pure build thanks to `Heart of the Blue-Eyes`
  (Rage of the Abyss).

### Memento

- New archetype (The Infinite Forbidden): `Mementotlan Shleepy`,
  `Mementomictlan Tecuhtlica - Creation King`.
- Results: became a one card starter deck with the Fiendsmith engine and `Aerial Eater`.

### Mermail

- New support (Rage of the Abyss): `Abyssrhine, the Atlantean Spirit`,
  `Mermail Shadow Squad`, `Poseidra Abyss, the Atlantean Dragon Lord`,
  `Mermail King - Neptabyss`.
- Results: top eight at YCS Barcelona, the largest non-WCQ event of the year (over 2500 duelists).

### Newer archetypes to watch

- Ryu-Ge (Crossover Breakers): `Kyoro Ryu-Ge Kaiva`, `Sosei Ryu-Ge Mistva`, `Ryu-Ge War Zone`,
  a splash engine for Dinosaur and Swordsoul rather than a standalone deck.
- Drytron (Cancun era): `Drytron Nu II`, `Drytron Meteonis DA Draconids`,
  turned into a boss focused strategy.
- Crystal Beast: `Advanced Crystal Beast Sapphire Pegasus`, `Golden Rule`,
  topped YCS Cancun in the hands of Jesse Cotton.
