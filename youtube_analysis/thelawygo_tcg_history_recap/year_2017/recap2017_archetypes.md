# 2017 TCG: Top Archetypes

Summary of [recap2017_transcript.md](recap2017_transcript.md)
(TheLawYGO, The 2017 Yugioh TCG Recap).

Top archetypes are ordered by how long they stayed a top deck, longest first.
Months are approximate: the video dates set releases and ban lists exactly,
but most events only relative to them ("two weeks later").

World Championship 2017 winner (Tokyo, Aug 12th to 13th): True King Dinosaur,
Ryosuke Tsujimura (Japan; heard as "Sujimura Rosoki", spelling checked externally),
listed externally as True King Yang Zing Dinosaur.
Worlds used the combined TCG and OCG ban list, where Zoodiac was already crippled,
under Master Rule 4 with only the Links of Starter Deck: Link Strike legal,
so decks that need one extra deck monster at a time were favored.

2017 sets in release order, for reference:
Pendulum Domination Structure Deck (Jan 20th), Raging Tempest (Feb 10th),
Fusion Enforcers (Feb 24th), Duelist Saga (Mar 31st),
Machine Reactor Structure Deck and Dinosmasher's Fury Structure Deck (Apr 14th),
Maximum Crisis (May 5th),
Pendulum Evolution (Jun 23rd), Battles of Legend: Light's Revenge (Jul 7th),
Starter Deck: Link Strike (Jul 21st), Code of the Duelist (Aug 4th),
Legendary Duelists (Sep 8th),
Circuit Break (Oct 20th), Spirit Warriors (Nov).

Master Rule 4 (Link monsters and the Extra Monster Zones)
started with Starter Deck: Link Strike in Jul.

## Zoodiac

- Top period: Feb to Sep (about 8 months), tier zero for almost all of it.
  Nearly every deck splashed the engine: 31 of the top 32 at YCS Seattle (Feb),
  32 of the top 32 at YCS Pittsburgh (May).
- New archetype:
  - Raging Tempest: `Zoodiac Ratpier`, `Zoodiac Thoroughblade`, `Zoodiac Whiptail`,
    `Zoodiac Ramram`, `Zoodiac Bunnyblast`, `Zoodiac Barrage`,
    and the Xyz `Zoodiac Boarbow`, `Zoodiac Tigermortar`, `Zoodiac Broadbull`, `Zoodiac Drident`.
  - Maximum Crisis: `Zoodiac Hammerkong`, `Zoodiac Chakanine`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - Ways to reach Ratpier without the normal summon: `Fire Formation - Tenki`,
    `Speedroid Terrortop` into `M-X-Saber Invoker`, and later `Predaplant Ophrys Scorpio`
    with `Predaplant Darlingtonia Cobra` searching `Instant Fusion`.
  - `Daigusto Emeral` as the draw at the end of the combo.
  - `Instant Fusion`, `Lunalight Black Sheep` and `Fusion Substitute`
    to fuse used Xyz into `Elder Entity Norden` (after Ratpier went to two).
  - `Missus Radiant` (Code of the Duelist) let it ignore Master Rule 4.
  - Partner engines that named the variants: Kaiju (`Interrupted Kaiju Slumber`),
    Artifact (`Artifact Moralltach`), Infernoid with `That Grass Looks Greener`,
    Metalfoes with `Baobaboon`, True Draco (Draco Zoodiac), Zombie.
  - `Lullaby of Obedience` and `Shuffle Reborn` in some winning lists.
- Results: won YCS Seattle (Kaiju variant), YCS Guatemala City (Metalfoes variant),
  YCS Denver (Infernoid variant), YCS Bochum (pure), YCS Pittsburgh (Draco variant),
  all four WCQs (Central America, Europe at 17-0, Oceania, South America),
  the North American WCQ (Draco variant), YCS Toronto (Draco variant) and YCS Rimini.
- Ended by: the Sep 18th ban list
  (Broadbull, Drident, Emeral banned, Ratpier limited).
  The Mar ban list (Ratpier to two) and Jun ban list (Norden banned) did not stop it.

## True Draco

- Top period: May to Sep (about 5 months),
  first as a splash in Zoodiac, then as the main deck with a Zoodiac splash.
- New archetype (Maximum Crisis): `Master Peace, the True Dracoslayer`,
  `Dinomight Knight, the True Dracofighter`, `Ignis Heat, the True Dracowarrior`,
  `Majesty Maiden, the True Dracocaster`, `Dragonic Diagram`, `True King's Return`,
  `True Draco Heritage`, `Disciples of the True Dracophoenix`, `True Draco Apocalypse`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Zoodiac Barrage` and leftover Zoodiac monsters as tribute fodder for Master Peace,
    making it immune to monsters and spells.
  - `Pot of Duality` and `Card of Demise`: their special summon locks don't matter
    to a deck that tribute summons.
  - `Forbidden Apocrypha` against Zoodiac combos,
    `The Monarchs Stormforth` as a quick tribute summon.
- Results: won YCS Pittsburgh as Draco Zoodiac (May),
  pure True Draco topped the Central America, European and Oceania WCQs,
  won the North American WCQ (Jul) and YCS Toronto (after Worlds),
  still topping YCS Guadalajara after the Sep ban list.
- Ended by: the Sep 18th ban list (Dinomight Knight banned, Ignis Heat limited)
  weakened it, then SPYRAL took over in Oct.
  Master Peace itself was never hit in 2017.

## Pendulum Magician

- Top period: Jul to Oct (about 4 months): first top in Jul, the top deck in Sep,
  still topping at YCS London in Oct.
- New strong support:
  - Pendulum Evolution: `Double Iris Magician`, `Black Fang Magician`, `White Wing Magician`,
    `Purple Poison Magician`, `Harmonizing Magician`, `Astrograph Sorcerer`, `Chronograph Sorcerer`,
    `Star Pendulumgraph`, `Time Pendulumgraph`, `Timebreaker Magician`.
  - Code of the Duelist: `Supreme King Dragon Odd-Eyes`, `Supreme King Dragon Dark Rebellion`,
    `Supreme King Dragon Clear Wing`, `Supreme King Dragon Starving Venom`.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - The Jun ban list unlimited `Performapal Skullcrobat Joker`, `Wisdom-Eye Magician`
    and `Pendulum Call`, a deliberate push for the WCQ season.
  - `Zoodiac Barrage` pops to trigger the Magicians (European WCQ).
  - `Duelist Alliance` (Maximum Crisis) to search Pendulum cards.
- Results: first top at the European WCQ (Jul),
  won YCS Guadalajara (Gabrielle Romo) after Zoodiac's ban (Sep),
  topped YCS London (Oct, a week before the Nov 6th ban list).

## SPYRAL

- Top period: Sep to Dec (about 4 months):
  a serious talking point after its first pure YCS top in Sep,
  then a new tier zero from Oct.
- New strong support:
  - Raging Tempest: `SPYRAL Tough`, `SPYRAL GEAR - Utility Wire`, `SPYRAL MISSION - Recapture`.
  - Maximum Crisis: `SPYRAL Sleeper`, `SPYRAL GEAR - Last Resort`, `SPYRAL GEAR - Fully Armed`,
    `SPYRAL MISSION - Rescue`.
  - Circuit Break: `SPYRAL Double Helix`, a Link that counts as `SPYRAL Super Agent`
    and gives quick access to `SPYRAL Master Plan`. The reason it became tier zero.
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - Links solved getting Master Plan on and off the field:
    `Firewall Dragon`, reached through `Blackwing - Gofu the Vague Shadow`
    and `SPYRAL Quik-Fix` with `Machine Duplication`.
  - `Lyrilusc - Recital Starling` to search `D.D. Crow`, `Sylvan Princessprout`.
  - `PSY-Framegear Gamma` against hand traps.
  - `Linkuriboh` (Dec promo): repeated Quik-Fix Link climbing,
    bringing it back to tier zero at the end of the year.
- Results: 29 of the top 32 at YCS Dallas (Oct), won by Imran Khan,
  27 of the top 32 at YCS London (Jesse Cotton),
  first pure YCS top at YCS Guadalajara (Sep),
  then won YCS San Diego and YCS Prague 2 (Nov to Dec).
- Not ended in 2017: the Nov 6th ban list limited Gofu, `SPYRAL GEAR - Drone`, Quik-Fix
  and `Set Rotation`, but it kept winning.

## Metalfoes

- Top period: Jan to Mar (about 3 months), carried over from late 2016.
- Results: most represented and winner at YCS Sydney (Michael Duke).
  After Raging Tempest it survived as Metalfoes Zoodiac (a 50 card top at YCS Seattle),
  won YCS Guatemala City in Mar with `Baobaboon` (Gerald South Chavez),
  then lost `Majespecter Unicorn - Kirin` in the Mar 31st ban list
  and fell back on `Qliphort Scout` into `Cyber Dragon Infinity`.

## Infernoid

- Top period: Feb to Apr (about 3 months), as Infernoid Zoodiac.
- Key non-archetype cards: `That Grass Looks Greener`, `Void Imagination`, `Left Arm Offering`.
- Results: most popular deck outside Zoodiac at YCS Seattle (Feb),
  and won YCS Denver as Infernoid Zoodiac (Apr, Anderson Saying).

## Kozmo

- Top period: Feb to Apr (about 3 months), as a Zoodiac variant, then Tin Can Turbo.
- New strong support: none of its own in 2017 (the archetype is from 2016).
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Zoodiac Barrage` to pop the ships for setup.
  - `Fire King Avatar Yaksha` for the ship pops (popping back up at YCS Sydney, Jan).
  - `Dimensional Barrier` to lock out the Xyz onslaught.
- Results: a Kozmo variant of Zoodiac in the YCS Seattle top 32 (Feb),
  back in the YCS Denver top cut (Apr) with a Tin Can Turbo list.

## Burning Abyss

- Top period: Feb, Oct (about 2 months), with a gap in between.
- Key non-archetype cards: `Absolute King Back Jack` had been experimented with
  to set up Fire Lake pushes.
- Results: the only top 32 deck without Zoodiac at YCS Seattle (60 card build, Feb),
  topped YCS London against SPYRAL by playing many starters (Oct).

## Invoked

- Top period: Mar, Oct (about 2 months), with a gap in between.
- New archetype (Fusion Enforcers): `Aleister the Invoker`, `Invocation`, `Magical Meltdown`,
  `Invoked Mechaba`, `Invoked Raidjin`, `Invoked Caliga`.
- Results: splashed into Zoodiac and Artifact Windwitch builds (YCS Atlanta, Mar),
  and one of the few decks to top against SPYRAL at YCS Dallas with `Set Rotation` (Oct).

## Trickstar

- Top period: Sep to Oct (about 2 months).
- New archetype (Code of the Duelist): `Trickstar Candina`, `Trickstar Lycoris`,
  `Trickstar Lilybell`, `Trickstar Holly Angel`, `Trickstar Light Stage`, `Trickstar Reincarnation`.
- Key non-archetype cards: the Windwitch engine for `Crystal Wing Synchro Dragon`,
  `Droll & Lock Bird` with Reincarnation, and a full suite of hand traps.
- Results: topped YCS Guadalajara (Sep) and YCS Dallas (Oct).

## Paleozoic

- Top period: Mar (about 1 month), the only deck to beat Zoodiac in its first weeks.
- New strong support: none of its own in 2017 (the archetype is from 2016).
- Key non-archetype cards:
  - `Toadally Awesome` and the Frog engine, carried over from 2016.
  - `Lost Wind` (Raging Tempest): a repeatable trap trigger for Paleozoic
    whose negation and attack halving stay, so it shuts down a Drident.
  - `That Grass Looks Greener` (Raging Tempest) in the 60 card version,
    with `Card of Demise`, `Absolute King Back Jack` and `Fiend Griefing`
    to turn the milled traps into triggers.
- Results: won YCS Atlanta (Cory Roa) and YCS Prague (Joshua Schmidt,
  the author of [josh_yugioh_improve](../../josh_yugioh_improve)), both in Mar.

## True King Dinosaur

- Top period: none in TCG events; in the table because it won the World Championship,
  played under the combined TCG and OCG ban list.
- New strong support: `Ultimate Conductor Tyranno`, `Souleating Oviraptor`, `Petiteranodon`,
  `Lost World` (Machine Reactor Structure Deck and Dinosmasher's Fury Structure Deck),
  `Miscellaneousaurus` and
  `True King Lithosagym, the Disaster` (Raging Tempest),
  `True King of All Calamities` (Maximum Crisis),
  `Giant Rex` and `Double Evolution Pill` (Battles of Legend: Light's Revenge).
- Results: a beloved budget rogue deck in the TCG, but won the World Championship (Aug).
- Ended by: the Sep 18th ban list
  (Lithosagym and Denglong banned, Miscellaneousaurus limited).

## Honorable mentions

Playable with strong new cards, but never won a major event in 2017
nor made the top cut at two or more.

### D/D/D

- New strong support (Pendulum Domination Structure Deck):
  `D/D Lamia`, `D/D Savant Copernicus`, `D/D Orthros`,
  `D/D/D Dragonbane King Beowulf`, `D/D/D Cursed King Siegfried`;
  later `D/D Ghost` (Maximum Crisis) and `D/D Vice Typhon` (Code of the Duelist).
- Held back by: delayed so long that Zoodiac arrived three weeks later,
  then by Master Rule 4.

### Newer archetypes to watch

- Gouki (Code of the Duelist, Circuit Break): `Gouki Suprex`, `Gouki Headbatt`,
  `Gouki The Great Ogre`, recognized as a strong Link deck.
- Altergeist (Circuit Break): `Altergeist Marionetter`, `Altergeist Meluseek`,
  `Altergeist Protocol`, promising but not there yet.
- Twilightsworn (Code of the Duelist): `Punishment Dragon`, `Twilight Twin Dragons`.
