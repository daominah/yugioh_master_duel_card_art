## Reviewed pairs

All pairs below looked suspicious but were confirmed as intended.

- [x] **Limiter Removal** (5129) → 19945: original is a speedometer pushed into the red zone;
  alt keeps the same gauge, now behind Cyber Dragons overloading in blue lightning.
- [x] **Reinforcement of the Army** (5328) → 20040: original shows a blonde male soldier leading troops;
  alt shows a dark-haired female warrior in colorful foliage.
  Both are warriors.
- [x] **Breaker the Magical Warrior** (5655) → 22081: original shows a red/gold armored warrior with staff;
  alt shows the same figure in an impressionistic style, with the dragon likely in the background.
- [x] **Junk Warrior** (7696) → 19077: original is a purple mechanical warrior;
  alt shows a silver/white armored warrior with wings in purple energy.
- [x] **Mask Change** (9066) → 21731: original shows a HERO dashing in a burst of light;
  alt shows a white cloaked figure with crystal/diamond effects. Styles are very different but confirmed.
- [x] **Tuning** (9122) → 19943: original shows a figure playing a harp;
  alt shows a cartoon character using a wrench on a record disc.
  The alt art depicts the most common Tuner target of the card effect.
- [x] **Medallion of the Ice Barrier** (9131) → 19736: original shows the blue medallion/coin;
  alt shows two Ice Barrier children characters.
- [x] **Masked HERO Dark Law** (11313) → 21730: original is a black armored HERO;
  alt shows a silver/metallic slashing figure in yellow energy. The head shapes look similar.
- [x] **Invocation** (12852) → 3432: original shows a blue aquatic creature being summoned;
  alt depicts a new monster that Invocation can summon.
- [x] **Called by the Grave** (13619) → 21233: original shows a dark graveyard scene;
  alt shows a bright colorful chibi-style scene. Both feature a pointing hand.
- [x] **I:P Masquerena** (14676) → 22746: original is the purple/yellow tech girl;
  alt shows a golden cat-like figure with the girl behind it.
- [x] **Lightning Storm** (14876) → 21235: original is pure yellow lightning striking bare rocks;
  alt shows that lightning tearing a machine dragon apart, the effect the card has on a field.
- [x] **Secrets of Dark Magic** (14908) → 21728: original shows a blue vortex with angel figures;
  alt shows a dark fiery sorcerer. Both feature ritual props such as candelabras.
- [x] **Fusion Deployment** (15057) → 22188: original shows a dark caped fusion warrior above armored soldiers;
  alt shows Albaz with a dragon gauntlet facing a horned fiend,
  from the same Albaz saga batch as 22186 and 22187.
- [x] **Fallen of Albaz** (15245) → 20567: original is a white/silver dragon knight;
  alt shows Albaz in human form with white hair and a fire sword.
- [x] **Blazing Cartesia, the Virtuous** (17767) → 20570: original is a dark armored figure with black/red wings;
  alt shows a white-haired multi-armed figure surrounded by flames.
- [x] **Triple Tactics Thrust** (18214) → 22189: original is a red-armored general on horseback;
  alt shows three characters instead, one for each tactic the card offers.
